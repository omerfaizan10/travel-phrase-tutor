# Spanish at the hotel, and telling the time and date

Source: Wikivoyage, "Spanish phrasebook" (Lodging, Time, Months, Writing time and date), https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spanish_phrasebook. CC BY-SA 4.0. Hotel phrases and dates. Also: Peace Corps Uruguay, "Español I: Manual" (1993), Unit 9 dialogue "En el Hotel", https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/spanish/courses/peace-corps-spanish-language-course/, public domain (Latin American usage, marked). Clock times: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Spain" (1984), Module II Unit 1 and keys, https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-headstart-for-spain-course/, public domain. Pronunciations follow Spain (z, ce, ci = "th").

## At reception

- ¿Hay habitaciones libres? [AY ah-bee-tah-THYOH-nehs LEE-brehs] = Do you have any rooms available?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta una habitación para una persona? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah OO-nah ah-bee-tah-THYOHN PAH-rah OO-nah pehr-SOH-nah] = How much is a room for one person?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta una habitación para dos personas? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah OO-nah ah-bee-tah-THYOHN PAH-rah dohs pehr-SOH-nahs] = How much is a room for two people?
- Me quedaré tres noches. [meh keh-dah-REH trehs NOH-chehs] = I will stay three nights.
- ¿Puedo ver la habitación primero? [PWEH-doh behr lah ah-bee-tah-THYOHN pree-MEH-roh] = May I see the room first?
- Muy bien, la tomaré. [MOO-ee byehn, lah toh-mah-REH] = OK, I'll take it.
- ¿Puede recomendarme otros hoteles? [PWEH-deh reh-koh-mehn-DAR-meh OH-trohs oh-TEH-lehs] = Can you suggest other hotels?

## What the receptionist says

- (receptionist) ¿Tiene reservación? [TYEH-neh rreh-sehr-bah-THYOHN] = Do you have a reservation? (from the Peace Corps Uruguay dialogue)
- (receptionist) ¿Cuánto tiempo piensa quedarse? [KWAHN-toh TYEHM-poh PYEHN-sah keh-DAR-seh] = How long are you planning to stay?
- Sólo por esta noche. [SOH-loh pohr EHS-tah NOH-cheh] = Just for tonight.
- (receptionist) Son ... por día. [sohn ... pohr DEE-ah] = It's ... per day.
- (receptionist) Es en el segundo piso, habitación 234. [ehs ehn ehl seh-GOON-doh PEE-soh, ah-bee-tah-THYOHN dohs-THYEHN-tohs TREHN-tah ee KWAH-troh] = It's on the second floor, room 234.
- ¿Qué número de habitación es? [keh NOO-meh-roh deh ah-bee-tah-THYOHN ehs] = What's the room number?
  Note: the Peace Corps dialogue says "una pieza individual" (a single room); pieza is Latin American. In Spain say habitación.

## The room

- ¿La habitación viene con un baño? [lah ah-bee-tah-THYOHN BYEH-neh kohn oon BAH-nyoh] = Does the room come with a bathroom?
- ¿Tiene baño privado? [TYEH-neh BAH-nyoh pree-BAH-doh] = Does it have a private bathroom?
- ¿La habitación viene con una cama de matrimonio? [lah ah-bee-tah-THYOHN BYEH-neh kohn OO-nah KAH-mah deh mah-tree-MOH-nyoh] = Does the room have a double bed?
- ¿Tiene algo más tranquilo? [TYEH-neh AHL-goh mahs trahn-KEE-loh] = Do you have anything quieter?
- ¿Tiene algo más barato? [TYEH-neh AHL-goh mahs bah-RAH-toh] = Do you have anything cheaper?
- ¿Hay caja fuerte? [AY KAH-hah FWEHR-teh] = Is there a safe?
- Por favor, limpie mi habitación. [pohr fah-BOHR, LEEM-pyeh mee ah-bee-tah-THYOHN] = Please clean my room.
- la habitación = room; la llave = key; la cama = bed; el baño = bathroom; el piso = floor (storey); el hotel = hotel; el hostal = hostel

## Breakfast, wake-up and checkout

- ¿El desayuno va incluido? [ehl deh-sah-YOO-noh bah een-kloo-EE-doh] = Is breakfast included?
- ¿A qué hora es el desayuno? [ah keh OH-rah ehs ehl deh-sah-YOO-noh] = What time is breakfast?
- ¿Puede despertarme a las siete? [PWEH-deh dehs-pehr-TAR-meh ah lahs SYEH-teh] = Can you wake me at seven?
- Quiero dejar el hotel. [KYEH-roh deh-HAR ehl oh-TEHL] = I want to check out.
- Sólo una maleta. [SOH-loh OO-nah mah-LEH-tah] = Only one suitcase.
  The FSI Cultural Notes (1984) say tourist hotels serve breakfast from about 8 to 11 am; check with your hotel.

## Telling the time

- ¿Qué hora es? [keh OH-rah ehs] = What time is it?
- ¿Puede decirme qué hora es? [PWEH-deh deh-THEER-meh keh OH-rah ehs] = Can you tell me what time it is?
- Es la una. [ehs lah OO-nah] = It's one o'clock.
- Son las dos. [sohn lahs dohs] = It's two o'clock.
- Son las siete y veinte. [sohn lahs SYEH-teh ee BAYN-teh] = It's 7:20.
- Son las doce y cuarto. [sohn lahs DOH-theh ee KWAR-toh] = It's a quarter past twelve.
- Son las cuatro y media. [sohn lahs KWAH-troh ee MEH-dyah] = It's half past four.
- Son las nueve menos cuarto. [sohn lahs NWEH-beh MEH-nohs KWAR-toh] = It's a quarter to nine.
- Son las diez menos veinticinco. [sohn lahs dyehth MEH-nohs bayn-tee-THEEN-koh] = It's twenty-five to ten.
- Es la una de la tarde. [ehs lah OO-nah deh lah TAR-deh] = It's one in the afternoon.
- a las once [ah lahs OHN-theh] = at eleven
- mediodía [meh-dyoh-DEE-ah] = noon; medianoche [meh-dyah-NOH-cheh] = midnight
  Es la una for one o'clock, son las for all other hours. In speech people use the 12-hour clock with de la mañana (morning), de la tarde (afternoon), de la noche (night). Written times (timetables, signs) usually use the 24-hour clock: 13:00 = la una de la tarde.

## Dates

- el 7 de mayo de 2003 = 7 May 2003 (day, then month, then year)
- mil novecientos noventa y siete = 1997 (years are said as whole numbers)
- hoy = today; ayer = yesterday; mañana = tomorrow
- esta semana = this week; la semana pasada = last week; la semana que viene = next week
- un minuto = a minute; una hora = an hour; un día = a day; una semana = a week; un mes = a month; un año = a year
- enero = January; febrero = February; marzo = March; abril = April; mayo = May; junio = June; julio = July; agosto = August; septiembre = September; octubre = October; noviembre = November; diciembre = December
- Hace dos semanas que estoy en España. [AH-theh dohs seh-MAH-nahs keh ehs-TOY ehn ehs-PAH-nyah] = I have been in Spain for two weeks.
- ¿Hace mucho que usted está aquí? [AH-theh MOO-choh keh oos-TEHD ehs-TAH ah-KEE] = Have you been here long? (formal)
  Months and days are written with a small letter.

## Saying the time out loud (Libro Libre)

Source: Erin Huebener, "Libro Libre: Beginning Spanish" (2018), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (this section is shared under the same licence), "La hora" (p. 76). English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- Son las dos y siete de la tarde. = It's 2:07 in the afternoon.
- Son las seis menos diez de la noche. = It's 5:50 in the evening (ten to six).
- Es la una. = It's one o'clock. (Es la for one o'clock; Son las for every other hour.)
- y cuarto = quarter past;  y media = half past;  menos cuarto = quarter to
- de la mañana = in the morning (am);  de la tarde = in the afternoon;  de la noche = at night
- el mediodía = noon;  la medianoche = midnight;  en punto = on the dot / exactly
- Note: printed schedules (trains, buses) use the 24-hour clock, but people say a train at 13:05 leaves "a la una y cinco", not "a las trece y cinco".
