# Spanish for talking about the weather and seasons

Source: Peace Corps Uruguay, "Español I: Manual (para Peace Corps Volunteers)" (1993), Unit 6 "El pronóstico del tiempo" (forecast text, questions, weather vocabulary, expressions with tener), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/spanish/courses/peace-corps-spanish-language-course/. US Government work, public domain. Also: Wikivoyage, "Spanish phrasebook" (Seasons), https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spanish_phrasebook, CC BY-SA 4.0; and Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Spain: Cultural Notes" (1984, climate), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-headstart-for-spain-course/, public domain. The weather words are the same in Spain and Latin America. Pronunciations are ours, following Spain (z, ce, ci = "th").

## Asking about the weather
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¿Qué tiempo hace? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh] = What's the weather like?
- ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh OY] = What's the weather like today?
- ¿Qué tiempo hizo ayer? [keh TYEHM-poh EE-thoh ah-YEHR] = What was the weather like yesterday?
- ¿Qué tiempo va a hacer mañana? [keh TYEHM-poh bah ah ah-THEHR mah-NYAH-nah] = What will the weather be like tomorrow?
- ¿Cuál es la temperatura máxima del lugar? [kwahl ehs lah tehm-peh-rah-TOO-rah MAHK-see-mah dehl loo-GAR] = What is the highest temperature here?
  Weather in Spanish uses hacer ("to make"): hace frío = it's cold, literally "it makes cold".

## Describing the weather
Country: ES (Spain)

- Hace frío. [AH-theh FREE-oh] = It's cold.
- Hace calor. [AH-theh kah-LOR] = It's hot.
- Hace mal tiempo. [AH-theh mahl TYEHM-poh] = The weather is bad.
- Está lloviendo. [ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh] = It's raining.
- Está nevando. [ehs-TAH neh-BAHN-doh] = It's snowing.
- Llueve. [YWEH-beh] = It rains.
- Nieva. [NYEH-bah] = It snows.
- Truena. [TRWEH-nah] = It's thundering.
- Hay sol. [AY sohl] = It's sunny.
- Hay nubes. [AY NOO-behs] = It's cloudy.
- seco = dry; húmedo = humid

## Weather words
Country: ALL (any Spanish-speaking country)

- el tiempo = the weather; el clima = the climate
- el sol = the sun; la lluvia = the rain; la nieve = the snow; el viento = the wind; la nube = the cloud
- la tormenta = the storm; el trueno = the thunder; el relámpago = the lightning; el granizo = the hail; la escarcha = the frost
- la temperatura = the temperature; grado centígrado = degree Celsius; la humedad = the humidity
- nublado = cloudy; despejado = clear (sky); fresco = cool; frío = cold
  Note: el tiempo means both "the weather" and "time".

## Understanding a forecast
Country: ALL (any Spanish-speaking country)

- el pronóstico del tiempo [ehl proh-NOHS-tee-koh dehl TYEHM-poh] = the weather forecast
- frío, cielo parcialmente nublado, vientos suaves y variables = cold, partly cloudy sky, light and variable winds
- nublado y frío, ligeras precipitaciones = cloudy and cold, light rain
- bueno y fresco, cielo despejado = fine and cool, clear sky
- una temperatura mínima de 8°C, y una máxima de 17°C = a low of 8°C and a high of 17°C
  Spain measures temperature in degrees Celsius (centígrados). To convert to Fahrenheit: multiply by 9, divide by 5, add 32.

## How you feel: cold, hot, ill
Country: ALL (any Spanish-speaking country)

- Tengo frío. [TEHN-goh FREE-oh] = I'm cold. (a person feels cold: tener frío)
- Tengo calor. [TEHN-goh kah-LOR] = I'm hot.
- tener frío = to be cold; tener calor = to be hot; tener sed = to be thirsty; tener hambre = to be hungry
- estar resfriado = to have a cold
  Use hace frío for the weather and tengo frío for how you feel.

## Seasons
Country: ES (Spain)

- la primavera [pree-mah-BEH-rah] = spring
- el verano [beh-RAH-noh] = summer
- el otoño [oh-TOH-nyoh] = autumn
- el invierno [een-BYEHR-noh] = winter
- una camisa de verano = a summer shirt; una chaqueta de invierno = a winter jacket

## Spain's climate
Country: ES (Spain)
- el norte = the north: rain all year, green, mild winters and cool summers
- la Meseta = the high central plateau around Madrid: cold winters with snow and wind, dry summers
- el sur = the south: hot, dry summers and pleasant winters
The Mediterranean coast and the Balearic Islands are sunny and warm with long dry spells, and brief heavy storms in spring and autumn. In summer many people from Madrid go to the cooler coasts.

## Weather with hace, está and hay (Libro Libre)
Country: ES (Spain)

Source: Erin Huebener, "Libro Libre: Beginning Spanish" (2018), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (this section is shared under the same licence), "El tiempo: ¿Qué tiempo hace?" (pp. 73-74). English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Qué tiempo hace? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh] = What's the weather like?
- ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh oy] = What's the weather like today?
- Hace (muy) buen tiempo. [AH-theh bwehn TYEHM-poh] = The weather is (very) good.
- Hace (muy) mal tiempo. [AH-theh mahl TYEHM-poh] = The weather is (very) bad.
- Hace calor. = It's hot.;  Hace frío. = It's cold.;  Hace sol. = It's sunny.;  Hace viento. = It's windy.;  Hace fresco. = It's cool.
- Está soleado. = It's sunny.;  Está nublado. = It's cloudy.;  Está despejado. = It's clear.
- Está lloviendo. [ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh] = It's raining (right now).;  Está nevando. = It's snowing (right now).
- Hay nubes. = There are clouds.;  Hay tormentas. = There are storms.;  Hay niebla. = It's foggy.
- Llueve. = It rains.;  Nieva. = It snows.
- La temperatura está a 20 grados. = The temperature is 20 degrees.
- Va a llover. [bah ah yoh-BEHR] = It's going to rain.
- ¡Qué calor! = It's so hot!;  ¡Qué frío! = It's so cold!

## Talking about the heat (Puerto Rico)
Country: PR (Puerto Rico)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Puerto Rico", Module I Unit 2 "In Puerto Rico", exercises 12-17 (pp. 30-31) and key (pp. 206-207), https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-puerto-rico. US Government work (DLI); no license statement in the PDF. English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Hace calor en Puerto Rico? [AH-seh kah-LOR ehn PWEHR-toh REE-koh] = Is it hot in Puerto Rico?
- Sí, hace mucho calor en Puerto Rico. [SEE, AH-seh MOO-choh kah-LOR ehn PWEHR-toh REE-koh] = Yes, it's very hot in Puerto Rico.
- No hace frío en Puerto Rico. [noh AH-seh FREE-oh ehn PWEHR-toh REE-koh] = It's not cold in Puerto Rico.
- Hace un poco de frío en El Yunque. [AH-seh oon POH-koh deh FREE-oh ehn ehl YOON-keh] = It's a little cold in El Yunque.
- Hace un poco de viento en Luquillo. [AH-seh oon POH-koh deh BYEHN-toh ehn loo-KEE-yoh] = It's a little windy in Luquillo.
- No hace mucho viento. [noh AH-seh MOO-choh BYEHN-toh] = It's not very windy.
- Me gusta Puerto Rico, pero hace un poco de calor. [meh GOOS-tah PWEHR-toh REE-koh, PEH-roh AH-seh oon POH-koh deh kah-LOR] = I like Puerto Rico, but it's a little hot.

## Talking about the weather (Paraguay)
Country: PY (Paraguay)

Source: Peace Corps, "Spanish I and Work Book (For Peace Corps Volunteers)", Unidad Cinco, "Pronóstico del tiempo" and "¿Cómo es el clima de Paraguay?" (p. 53), https://www.livelingua.com/course/peace-corps/spanish-language-lessons---paraguay. Peace Corps (US Government) training material; the ERIC copy states it is federally funded or otherwise in the public domain. English glosses and pronunciations are ours (Latin American: z/ce/ci = "s", ll/y = "y").

- ¿Cómo es el clima de Paraguay? [KOH-moh ehs ehl KLEE-mah deh pah-rah-GWAY] = What is the climate like in Paraguay?
- No sé, el tiempo está muy feo aquí, está lloviendo y hace mucho frío. [noh SEH, ehl TYEHM-poh ehs-TAH MOO-ee FEH-oh ah-KEE, ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh ee AH-seh MOO-choh FREE-oh] = I don't know, the weather is awful here, it's raining and very cold.
- Aquí sin embargo hace mucho tiempo que no llueve, hay mucho polvo. [ah-KEE seen ehm-BAR-goh AH-seh MOO-choh TYEHM-poh keh noh YWEH-beh, ay MOO-choh POHL-boh] = Here, though, it hasn't rained for a long time; there's a lot of dust.
- aquí el clima está loco [ah-KEE ehl KLEE-mah ehs-TAH LOH-koh] = here the weather is crazy
- Bueno, si el tiempo mejora, vení. [BWEH-noh, see ehl TYEHM-poh meh-HOH-rah, beh-NEE] = Well, if the weather gets better, come. (vos command)
- cielo despejado [SYEH-loh dehs-peh-HAH-doh] = clear sky
- parcialmente nublado [par-syahl-MEHN-teh noo-BLAH-doh] = partly cloudy
- ligeras precipitaciones [lee-HEH-rahs preh-see-pee-tah-SYOH-nehs] = light rain
- vientos suaves y variables [BYEHN-tohs SWAH-behs ee bah-RYAH-blehs] = light, variable winds

## Climate words from speakers in Cuba (Cuba)
Country: CU (Cuba)

Source: words used by speakers from Cuba in Spanish Listening lesson #417 "Describe el clima de tu país" (https://www.spanishlistening.org/content/417-manu-cuba-clima.html). © Spanish Listening 2022; no license, so no transcript text is copied. Example phrases, glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- un clima tropical [oon KLEE-mah troh-pee-KAHL] = a tropical climate
- cálido / cálida [KAH-lee-doh / KAH-lee-dah] = warm
- un calor intenso [oon kah-LOHR een-TEHN-soh] = intense heat
- las lluvias [lahs YOO-byahs] = the rains, the rainy season
- el ciclón [ehl see-KLOHN] = the cyclone, the hurricane (Cubans often say ciclón)
- Depende de la región. [deh-PEHN-deh deh lah rreh-HYOHN] = It depends on the region.
- ¿Hay peligro de ciclones ahora? [ay peh-LEE-groh deh see-KLOH-nehs ah-OH-rah] = Is there a risk of hurricanes now?
  The speaker says the rains start around April-May, heat is intense until October, and cyclones can appear in that period; western Cuba is cooler from November to March. Check current forecasts before travelling.

## Forecast words from speakers in Spain (Spain)
Country: ES (Spain)

Source: words used by speakers from Spain in Spanish Listening lessons #029 "Previsión meteorológica" and #389 "¿Cuál es la mejor época del año para visitar España?" (https://www.spanishlistening.org/content/029-amaya-spain-forcast.html, https://www.spanishlistening.org/content/389-ramsey-espana-travel.html). © Spanish Listening 2022; no license, so no transcript text is copied. Example phrases, glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- la previsión meteorológica [lah preh-bee-SYOHN meh-teh-oh-roh-LOH-hee-kah] = the weather forecast
- ¿Qué dice la previsión para mañana? [keh DEE-theh lah preh-bee-SYOHN PAH-rah mah-NYAH-nah] = What does the forecast say for tomorrow?
- Va a hacer frío. [bah ah ah-THEHR FREE-oh] = It's going to be cold.
- unas temperaturas templadas [OO-nahs tehm-peh-rah-TOO-rahs tehm-PLAH-dahs] = mild temperatures
- las heladas [lahs eh-LAH-dahs] = frost
- Abrígate bien. [ah-BREE-gah-teh byehn] = Wrap up warm. (informal; abrigarse = to wrap up)
- el paraguas [ehl pah-RAH-gwahs] = the umbrella
- Coge el paraguas. [KOH-heh ehl pah-RAH-gwahs] = Take the umbrella. (coger = to take, in Spain)
- la mejor época para visitar [lah meh-HOR EH-poh-kah PAH-rah bee-see-TAR] = the best time (of year) to visit

## Heat and humidity: words from speakers in Argentina (Argentina)
Country: AR (Argentina)

Source: words used by speakers from Argentina in Spanish Listening lessons #134 "¿Cómo está el clima hoy?", #173 "¿Cómo está el tiempo hoy aquí?" (https://www.spanishlistening.org/content/134-Florencia_Argentina_Weather.html, https://www.spanishlistening.org/content/173-juan_argentina_weather.html). © Spanish Listening 2022; no license, so no transcript text is copied. Example phrases, glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- Está muy pesado. [ehs-TAH MOO-ee peh-SAH-doh] = It's very muggy. (heavy, humid heat)
- la sensación térmica [lah sehn-sah-SYOHN TEHR-mee-kah] = the "feels like" temperature
- un día caluroso [oon DEE-ah kah-loo-ROH-soh] = a hot day
- Hay mucha humedad. [AY MOO-chah oo-meh-DAHD] = It's very humid.
- una máxima de 40 grados [OO-nah MAHK-see-mah deh kwah-REHN-tah GRAH-dohs] = a high of 40 degrees
  Seasons are reversed in Argentina: in Buenos Aires January is midsummer, and summer heat comes with high humidity.
