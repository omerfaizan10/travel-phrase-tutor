# Spanish for talking about the weather and seasons

Source: Peace Corps Uruguay, "Español I: Manual (para Peace Corps Volunteers)" (1993), Unit 6 "El pronóstico del tiempo" (forecast text, questions, weather vocabulary, expressions with tener), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/spanish/courses/peace-corps-spanish-language-course/. US Government work, public domain. Also: Wikivoyage, "Spanish phrasebook" (Seasons), https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spanish_phrasebook, CC BY-SA 4.0; and Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Spain: Cultural Notes" (1984, climate), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-headstart-for-spain-course/, public domain. The weather words are the same in Spain and Latin America. Pronunciations are ours, following Spain (z, ce, ci = "th").

## Asking about the weather

- ¿Qué tiempo hace? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh] = What's the weather like?
- ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh OY] = What's the weather like today?
- ¿Qué tiempo hizo ayer? [keh TYEHM-poh EE-thoh ah-YEHR] = What was the weather like yesterday?
- ¿Qué tiempo va a hacer mañana? [keh TYEHM-poh bah ah ah-THEHR mah-NYAH-nah] = What will the weather be like tomorrow?
- ¿Cuál es la temperatura máxima del lugar? [kwahl ehs lah tehm-peh-rah-TOO-rah MAHK-see-mah dehl loo-GAR] = What is the highest temperature here?
  Weather in Spanish uses hacer ("to make"): hace frío = it's cold, literally "it makes cold".

## Describing the weather

- Hace frío. [AH-theh FREE-oh] = It's cold.
- Hace calor. [AH-theh kah-LOR] = It's hot.
- Hace mal tiempo. [AH-theh mahl TYEHM-poh] = The weather is bad.
- Está lloviendo. [ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh] = It's raining.
- Está nevando. [ehs-TAH neh-BAHN-doh] = It's snowing.
- Llueve. [YWEH-beh] = It rains.
- Nieva. [NYEH-bah] = It snows.
- Truena. [TRWEH-nah] = It's thundering.
- Hay sol. [AY sohl] = It's sunny.
- Hay nubes. [AY NOO-behs] = It's cloudy.
- seco = dry; húmedo = humid

## Weather words

- el tiempo = the weather; el clima = the climate
- el sol = the sun; la lluvia = the rain; la nieve = the snow; el viento = the wind; la nube = the cloud
- la tormenta = the storm; el trueno = the thunder; el relámpago = the lightning; el granizo = the hail; la escarcha = the frost
- la temperatura = the temperature; grado centígrado = degree Celsius; la humedad = the humidity
- nublado = cloudy; despejado = clear (sky); fresco = cool; frío = cold
  Note: el tiempo means both "the weather" and "time".

## Understanding a forecast

- el pronóstico del tiempo [ehl proh-NOHS-tee-koh dehl TYEHM-poh] = the weather forecast
- frío, cielo parcialmente nublado, vientos suaves y variables = cold, partly cloudy sky, light and variable winds
- nublado y frío, ligeras precipitaciones = cloudy and cold, light rain
- bueno y fresco, cielo despejado = fine and cool, clear sky
- una temperatura mínima de 8°C, y una máxima de 17°C = a low of 8°C and a high of 17°C
  Spain measures temperature in degrees Celsius (centígrados). To convert to Fahrenheit: multiply by 9, divide by 5, add 32.

## How you feel: cold, hot, ill

- Tengo frío. [TEHN-goh FREE-oh] = I'm cold. (a person feels cold: tener frío)
- Tengo calor. [TEHN-goh kah-LOR] = I'm hot.
- tener frío = to be cold; tener calor = to be hot; tener sed = to be thirsty; tener hambre = to be hungry
- estar resfriado = to have a cold
  Use hace frío for the weather and tengo frío for how you feel.

## Seasons

- la primavera [pree-mah-BEH-rah] = spring
- el verano [beh-RAH-noh] = summer
- el otoño [oh-TOH-nyoh] = autumn
- el invierno [een-BYEHR-noh] = winter
- una camisa de verano = a summer shirt; una chaqueta de invierno = a winter jacket

## Spain's climate
- el norte = the north: rain all year, green, mild winters and cool summers
- la Meseta = the high central plateau around Madrid: cold winters with snow and wind, dry summers
- el sur = the south: hot, dry summers and pleasant winters
The Mediterranean coast and the Balearic Islands are sunny and warm with long dry spells, and brief heavy storms in spring and autumn. In summer many people from Madrid go to the cooler coasts.

## Weather with hace, está and hay (Libro Libre)

Source: Erin Huebener, "Libro Libre: Beginning Spanish" (2018), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (this section is shared under the same licence), "El tiempo: ¿Qué tiempo hace?" (pp. 73-74). English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Qué tiempo hace? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh] = What's the weather like?
- ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? [keh TYEHM-poh AH-theh oy] = What's the weather like today?
- Hace (muy) buen tiempo. [AH-theh bwehn TYEHM-poh] = The weather is (very) good.
- Hace (muy) mal tiempo. [AH-theh mahl TYEHM-poh] = The weather is (very) bad.
- Hace calor. = It's hot.;  Hace frío. = It's cold.;  Hace sol. = It's sunny.;  Hace viento. = It's windy.;  Hace fresco. = It's cool.
- Está soleado. = It's sunny.;  Está nublado. = It's cloudy.;  Está despejado. = It's clear.
- Está lloviendo. [ehs-TAH yoh-BYEHN-doh] = It's raining (right now).;  Está nevando. = It's snowing (right now).
- Hay nubes. = There are clouds.;  Hay tormentas. = There are storms.;  Hay niebla. = It's foggy.
- Llueve. = It rains.;  Nieva. = It snows.
- La temperatura está a 20 grados. = The temperature is 20 degrees.
- Va a llover. [bah ah yoh-BEHR] = It's going to rain.
- ¡Qué calor! = It's so hot!;  ¡Qué frío! = It's so cold!
