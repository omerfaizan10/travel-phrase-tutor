Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Spain" (1984), Module II Unit 1 "En la Estación de Trenes" (dialogue, station signs, exercises, keys) and the "Cultural Notes" (trains), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-headstart-for-spain-course/. US Government work, public domain. Also: Wikivoyage, "Spanish phrasebook" (Bus and train, At the airport) and "Spain" (Get around: by train), https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spanish_phrasebook and https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spain. ## Finding the station

- Perdón, ¿puede usted decirme dónde está la estación de Chamartín? [pehr-DOHN, PWEH-deh oos-TEHD deh-THEER-meh DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh chah-mar-TEEN] = Excuse me, can you tell me where Chamartín station is?
- ¿Dónde está la estación de Atocha? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh ah-TOH-chah] = Where is Atocha station?
- ¿Cómo puedo llegar a la estación de tren? [KOH-moh PWEH-doh yeh-GAR ah lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh trehn] = How do I get to the train station?
- la estación de trenes = the train station
  Chamartín and Atocha are the two main railway stations in Madrid (both appear in the FSI course).

## Station signs

- HORARIO DE TRENES [oh-RAH-ryoh deh TREH-nehs] = train timetable
- SALIDAS [sah-LEE-dahs] = departures
- LLEGADAS [yeh-GAH-dahs] = arrivals
- ACCESO A LOS ANDENES [ahk-THEH-soh ah lohs ahn-DEH-nehs] = to the platforms (el andén: the platform)
- DESPACHO DE BILLETES [dehs-PAH-choh deh bee-YEH-tehs] = ticket office
- INFORMACIÓN [een-for-mah-THYOHN] = information
- SALA DE ESPERA [SAH-lah deh ehs-PEH-rah] = waiting room
- CONSIGNA [kohn-SEEG-nah] = left luggage
- RECLAMACIONES [rreh-klah-mah-THYOH-nehs] = complaints / customer claims office
- SERVICIOS [sehr-BEE-thyohs] = toilets
- ENTRADA = entrance; SALIDA = exit
  Note: SALIDA on its own means "exit"; SALIDAS on a board means "departures".

## Asking when the train leaves and arrives

- ¿A qué hora sale el próximo tren para Zaragoza? [ah keh OH-rah SAH-leh ehl PROHK-see-moh trehn PAH-rah thah-rah-GOH-thah] = What time does the next train to Zaragoza leave?
- ¿Cuándo sale el tren? [KWAHN-doh SAH-leh ehl trehn] = When does the train leave?
- ¿Cuándo llega el tren? [KWAHN-doh YEH-gah ehl trehn] = When does the train arrive?
- ¿A qué hora llega a Zaragoza? [ah keh OH-rah YEH-gah ah thah-rah-GOH-thah] = What time does it arrive in Zaragoza?
- ¿A qué hora llega el tren? [ah keh OH-rah YEH-gah ehl trehn] = What time does the train arrive?
- ¿Cuándo llegará este tren a Sevilla? [KWAHN-doh yeh-gah-RAH EHS-teh trehn ah seh-BEE-yah] = When will this train arrive in Seville?
- ¿Dónde está el tren hacia Toledo? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl trehn AH-thyah toh-LEH-doh] = Where is the train to Toledo?
- ¿Adónde va este tren? [ah-DOHN-deh bah EHS-teh trehn] = Where does this train go?
- ¿Se para este tren en Córdoba? [seh PAH-rah EHS-teh trehn ehn KOR-doh-bah] = Does this train stop in Córdoba?
- (clerk) Sale a las ... [SAH-leh ah lahs] = It leaves at ...
- (clerk) Llega a las ... [YEH-gah ah lahs] = It arrives at ...
- salir = to leave / depart; llegar = to arrive; la salida = departure; la llegada = arrival; el próximo tren = the next train
  Do not guess times: read the departures board (SALIDAS) or ask at INFORMACIÓN. Timetables are written in the 24-hour clock.

## Buying a ticket

- Un billete de ida y vuelta para Zaragoza. [oon bee-YEH-teh deh EE-dah ee BWEHL-tah PAH-rah thah-rah-GOH-thah] = A return ticket to Zaragoza.
- Un billete de ida para Toledo. [oon bee-YEH-teh deh EE-dah PAH-rah toh-LEH-doh] = A one-way ticket to Toledo.
- Un billete a Sevilla, por favor. [oon bee-YEH-teh ah seh-BEE-yah, pohr fah-BOHR] = One ticket to Seville, please.
- ¿Cuánto cuesta un billete a Barcelona? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah oon bee-YEH-teh ah bar-theh-LOH-nah] = How much is a ticket to Barcelona?
- (clerk) ¿Primera o segunda? [pree-MEH-rah oh seh-GOON-dah] = First or second class?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta primera? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah pree-MEH-rah] = How much is first class?
- Primera, por favor. [pree-MEH-rah, pohr fah-BOHR] = First, please.
- Muchas gracias. [MOO-chahs GRAH-thyahs] = Thank you very much.
- el billete = ticket (Spain); de ida = one-way; de ida y vuelta = return; primera = first class; segunda = second class
  In Latin America a ticket is a pasaje or boleto; in Spain it is a billete.

## Dialogue: at the ticket window (FSI)

- Viajero: ¿A qué hora sale el próximo tren para Zaragoza? = Traveller: What time does the next train to Zaragoza leave?
- Viajero: Un billete de ida y vuelta para Zaragoza. = Traveller: A return ticket to Zaragoza.
- Taquillero: ¿Primera o segunda? = Ticket clerk: First or second?
- Viajero: ¿Cuánto cuesta primera? = Traveller: How much is first class?
- Viajero: Primera, por favor. ¿A qué hora llega a Zaragoza? = Traveller: First, please. What time does it arrive in Zaragoza?
  The clerk's answers with times are left out on purpose: real times must come from the departures board or the clerk.

## Trains in Spain

- Renfe = the Spanish national rail company (runs most commuter, regional and long-distance trains)
- Adif = runs most stations; its website and app show departures and platforms
- AVE = high-speed trains (Madrid to Barcelona, Andalusia, Valencia and more)
- Cercanías = suburban / commuter trains (Rodalies in Catalonia)
- Media Distancia = regional trains, slower and usually cheaper
  Most lines radiate from Madrid, so between two nearby cities on different lines the bus can be quicker. Long-distance tickets should be bought in advance, and prices can change like airline fares.
