## Asking where something is
Country: ES (Spain)

- Perdón, ¿puede usted decirme dónde está la estación de Chamartín? [pehr-DOHN, PWEH-deh oos-TEHD deh-THEER-meh DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh chah-mar-TEEN] = Excuse me, can you tell me where the Chamartín station is?
- ¿Puede decirme dónde está la Plaza Mayor? [PWEH-deh deh-THEER-meh DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah PLAH-thah mah-YOR] = Can you tell me where the Plaza Mayor is?
- ¿Dónde está el restaurante América? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl rehs-tow-RAHN-teh ah-MEH-ree-kah] = Where is the América restaurant?
- ¿Dónde queda el restaurante El Bodegón? [DOHN-deh KEH-dah ehl rehs-tow-RAHN-teh ehl boh-deh-GOHN] = Where is El Bodegón restaurant (located)?
- ¿Hay un hotel cerca de aquí? [AY oon oh-TEHL THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Is there a hotel near here?
- ¿Hay un restaurante cerca de aquí? [AY oon rehs-tow-RAHN-teh THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Is there a restaurant near here?
- Perdone, agente, ¿hay una gasolinera cerca de aquí? [pehr-DOH-neh, ah-HEHN-teh, AY OO-nah gah-soh-lee-NEH-rah THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Excuse me, officer, is there a petrol station near here? (Address a police officer in Spain as agente.)
- ¿Y dónde está eso? [ee DOHN-deh ehs-TAH EH-soh] = And where is that?
## Asking how to get there
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¿Queda lejos? [KEH-dah LEH-hohs] = Is it far?
- ¿Cómo puedo llegar a la estación de tren? [KOH-moh PWEH-doh yeh-GAR ah lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh trehn] = How do I get to the train station?
- ¿Cómo puedo llegar al centro? [KOH-moh PWEH-doh yeh-GAR ahl THEHN-troh] = How do I get downtown?
- ¿Puede enseñarme en el mapa? [PWEH-deh ehn-seh-NYAR-meh ehn ehl MAH-pah] = Can you show me on the map?
- ¿Dónde están los aseos? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAHN lohs ah-SEH-ohs] = Where are the toilets? (Spain. Elsewhere: ¿Dónde está el baño?)

## Understanding the answer
Country: ES (Spain)

- (local) Sí, mire. [SEE, MEE-reh] = Yes, look.
- (local) En la próxima esquina tuerza a la izquierda y siga derecho. [ehn lah PROHK-see-mah ehs-KEE-nah TWEHR-thah ah lah eeth-KYEHR-dah ee SEE-gah deh-REH-choh] = At the next corner turn left and continue straight ahead.
- (local) La estación queda al final de la calle. [lah ehs-tah-THYOHN KEH-dah ahl fee-NAHL deh lah KAH-yeh] = The station is at the end of the street.
- (local) En el próximo semáforo, gire a la derecha. [ehn ehl PROHK-see-moh seh-MAH-foh-roh, HEE-reh ah lah deh-REH-chah] = At the next traffic light, turn right.
- (local) Entonces, siga derecho hasta encontrar la gasolinera. Está en una esquina. [ehn-TOHN-thehs, SEE-gah deh-REH-choh AHS-tah ehn-kohn-TRAR lah gah-soh-lee-NEH-rah. ehs-TAH ehn OO-nah ehs-KEE-nah] = Then continue straight ahead until you find the petrol station. It's on a corner.
## Understanding the answer: more examples
Country: ES (Spain)

- (local) Siga derecho por esta calle. [SEE-gah deh-REH-choh pohr EHS-tah KAH-yeh] = Continue straight ahead down this street.
- (local) Tuerza a la derecha y siga derecho por esta calle. [TWEHR-thah ah lah deh-REH-chah ee SEE-gah deh-REH-choh pohr EHS-tah KAH-yeh] = Turn right and continue straight down this street.
- (local) Siga derecho hasta el semáforo y gire a la izquierda. [SEE-gah deh-REH-choh AHS-tah ehl seh-MAH-foh-roh ee HEE-reh ah lah eeth-KYEHR-dah] = Continue straight ahead to the traffic light and turn left.
- (local) Gire a la izquierda en esa esquina. El restaurante está a la derecha. [HEE-reh ah lah eeth-KYEHR-dah ehn EH-sah ehs-KEE-nah. ehl rehs-tow-RAHN-teh ehs-TAH ah lah deh-REH-chah] = Turn left at that corner. The restaurant is on the right.
- (local) Sí, hay uno en la esquina. [SEE, AY OO-noh ehn lah ehs-KEE-nah] = Yes, there is one on the corner.
- (local) No, no hay ninguno cerca de aquí. [noh, noh AY neen-GOO-noh THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = No, there isn't one near here.
  Tuerza, gire and siga are polite (usted) commands. To several people: tuerzan, giren, sigan.

## Near or far
Country: ES (Spain)

- (local) Pues no. A dos o tres manzanas de aquí. [pwehs noh. ah dohs oh trehs mahn-THAH-nahs deh ah-KEE] = Well, no. Two or three blocks from here.
- (local) Queda a dos manzanas de aquí. [KEH-dah ah dohs mahn-THAH-nahs deh ah-KEE] = It's two blocks from here.
- (local) Esa gasolinera queda a tres kilómetros de aquí. [EH-sah gah-soh-lee-NEH-rah KEH-dah ah trehs kee-LOH-meh-trohs deh ah-KEE] = That petrol station is three kilometres from here.
- ¿Queda lejos de aquí ese taller? [KEH-dah LEH-hohs deh ah-KEE EH-seh tah-YEHR] = Is that garage far from here?
- cerca de aquí = near here; lejos = far; aquí = here
  In Spain a city block is una manzana. In Latin America it is una cuadra (¿A cuántas cuadras? = How many blocks away?).

## Direction words
Country: ES (Spain)

- a la izquierda [ah lah eeth-KYEHR-dah] = to the left
- a la derecha [ah lah deh-REH-chah] = to the right
- siga derecho / todo recto [SEE-gah deh-REH-choh / TOH-doh REHK-toh] = straight ahead
- tuerza / gire [TWEHR-thah / HEE-reh] = turn (Spain. Latin America often says doble.)
- la próxima esquina [lah PROHK-see-mah ehs-KEE-nah] = the next corner
- al final de la calle [ahl fee-NAHL deh lah KAH-yeh] = at the end of the street
- hasta [AHS-tah] = up to, until
- hacia el / la ... [AH-thyah] = towards the ...
- pasado el / la ... [pah-SAH-doh] = past the ...
- antes de ... [AHN-tehs deh] = before ...
- norte = north; sur = south; este = east; oeste = west

## Places in town
Country: ES (Spain)

- la estación de trenes = the train station; la estación de autobuses = the bus station; el aeropuerto = the airport
- el hotel = the hotel; el restaurante = the restaurant; la gasolinera = the petrol station; el aparcamiento = the car park
- la calle = the street; la avenida = the avenue; el paseo = the boulevard; la plaza = the square
- la esquina = the corner; el semáforo = the traffic light; la manzana = the block (Spain); el parque = the park
- el taller mecánico = the car repair shop; el hospital = the hospital; la embajada = the embassy
- el centro = the town centre / downtown
- el cruce = the crossroads

## Signs
Country: ES (Spain)

- ENTRADA = entrance; SALIDA = exit; ABIERTO = open; CERRADO = closed
- SERVICIOS / ASEOS = toilets; CABALLEROS / HOMBRES = men; SEÑORAS / MUJERES = women
- PROHIBIDO = forbidden; NO FUMAR = no smoking; EMPUJE = push; TIRE = pull

## Thanking someone
Country: ES (Spain)

- Muchas gracias. [MOO-chahs GRAH-thyahs] = Thank you very much.
- Muchas gracias, señora. [MOO-chahs GRAH-thyahs, seh-NYOH-rah] = Thank you very much, madam.
- Muchas gracias por la información. Es usted muy amable. [MOO-chahs GRAH-thyahs pohr lah een-for-mah-THYOHN. ehs oos-TEHD MOO-ee ah-MAH-bleh] = Thank you very much for the information. You are very kind.
- (local) De nada. [deh NAH-dah] = You're welcome.
- Estoy perdido. (m.) / Estoy perdida. (f.) [ehs-TOY pehr-DEE-doh / pehr-DEE-dah] = I'm lost.

## Asking the way, blocks and turns (Latin America)
Country: 419 (Unspecified Latin America)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Latin America Headstart Course", Module II Unit 2 "Is There a Gas Station Near Here?" and Unit 3 "Is There an Auto Shop Near Here?" (pp. 74-76, 94-96) and Module II Glossary (pp. 116-124); set in Panama, https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-latin-america. US Government work (DLI); no license statement in the PDF. English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Cómo hago para llegar allí? [KOH-moh AH-goh PAH-rah yeh-GAHR ah-YEE] = How do I get there? (literally "What do I do to get there?")
- (local) Vire a la derecha en esa esquina y usted verá la gasolinera. [BEE-reh ah lah deh-REH-chah ehn EH-sah ehs-KEE-nah ee oos-TEHD beh-RAH lah gah-soh-lee-NEH-rah] = Turn right at that corner and you will see the gas station.
- Muy agradecido, señor guardia. [mooy ah-grah-deh-SEE-doh, seh-NYOHR GWAHR-dyah] = Thank you very much, officer. (In Panama, address a police officer as señor guardia.)
- ¿A qué distancia de aquí queda ese taller? [ah keh dees-TAHN-syah deh ah-KEE KEH-dah EH-seh tah-YEHR] = How far from here is that repair shop?
- (local) Queda a seis cuadras de aquí, más o menos. [KEH-dah ah says KWAH-drahs deh ah-KEE, mahs oh MEH-nohs] = It's about six blocks from here.
- ¿A qué distancia? [ah keh dees-TAHN-syah] = How far?
- Doble. / Vire. [DOH-bleh / BEE-reh] = Turn. (to one person)
- Siga. [SEE-gah] = Continue. Keep going.
- derecho [deh-REH-choh] = straight ahead
- por esa calle [pohr EH-sah KAH-yeh] = down that street
- hasta el final [AHS-tah ehl fee-NAHL] = to the end
- la cuadra [lah KWAH-drah] = block (in a city)
- cerca de [SEHR-kah deh] = near
- lejos de [LEH-hohs deh] = far from
- más despacio [mahs dehs-PAH-syoh] = slower

## Asking the way (Puerto Rico)
Country: PR (Puerto Rico)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Puerto Rico", Module II Units 1-3 (conversations pp. 59, 75, 93; notes pp. 60, 76, 94; exercises pp. 62-63, 80; keys pp. 211-219), https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-puerto-rico. US Government work (DLI); no license statement in the PDF. English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Está muy lejos la plaza? [ehs-TAH MOO-ee LEH-hohs lah PLAH-sah] = Is the plaza very far?
- (local) Mire, en la próxima esquina doble a la izquierda, y siga directo por esa calle hasta el final. [MEE-reh, ehn lah PROHK-see-mah ehs-KEE-nah DOH-bleh ah lah ees-KYEHR-dah, ee SEE-gah dee-REHK-toh pohr EH-sah KAH-yeh AHS-tah ehl fee-NAHL] = Look, at the next corner turn left, and go straight down that street to the end.
- Señor policía, ¿hay una estación de gasolina cerca de aquí? [seh-NYOR poh-lee-SEE-ah, AY OO-nah ehs-tah-SYOHN deh gah-soh-LEE-nah SEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Officer, is there a gas station near here?
- ¿Cómo hago para llegar allí? [KOH-moh AH-goh PAH-rah yeh-GAR ah-YEE] = How do I get there?
- ¿Cómo hago para llegar al terminal de guaguas? [KOH-moh AH-goh PAH-rah yeh-GAR ahl tehr-mee-NAHL deh GWAH-gwahs] = How do I get to the bus terminal?
- (local) Vire a la derecha en esa esquina y usted verá la estación de gasolina. [BEE-reh ah lah deh-REH-chah ehn EH-sah ehs-KEE-nah ee oos-TEHD beh-RAH lah ehs-tah-SYOHN deh gah-soh-LEE-nah] = Turn right at that corner and you'll see the gas station.
- (local) Doble a la izquierda. [DOH-bleh ah lah ees-KYEHR-dah] = Turn left.
- (local) Siga derecho por esa calle hasta el final. [SEE-gah deh-REH-choh pohr EH-sah KAH-yeh AHS-tah ehl fee-NAHL] = Continue straight ahead down that street to the end.
- (local) Siga directo por esa carretera. [SEE-gah dee-REHK-toh pohr EH-sah kah-rreh-TEH-rah] = Continue straight ahead down that highway.
- (local) Usted verá la parada de guaguas. [oos-TEHD beh-RAH lah pah-RAH-dah deh GWAH-gwahs] = You'll see the bus stop.
- ¿A qué distancia? [ah KEH dees-TAHN-syah] = How far?
- (local) Está a dos kilómetros de aquí, más o menos. [ehs-TAH ah dohs kee-LOH-meh-trohs deh ah-KEE, MAHS oh MEH-nohs] = It's about two kilometers from here.
- (local) La playa queda a seis kilómetros de aquí, más o menos. [lah PLAH-yah KEH-dah ah SAYS kee-LOH-meh-trohs deh ah-KEE, MAHS oh MEH-nohs] = The beach is about six kilometers from here.
- (local) Está a ocho calles de aquí, más o menos. [ehs-TAH ah OH-choh KAH-yehs deh ah-KEE, MAHS oh MEH-nohs] = It's about eight streets from here.
- Muy amable, señora. [MOO-ee ah-MAH-bleh, seh-NYOH-rah] = That's very kind, ma'am.
- Muchas gracias, señor policía. [MOO-chahs GRAH-syahs, seh-NYOR poh-lee-SEE-ah] = Thank you very much, officer.
  In Puerto Rico you address a police officer as señor policía, the equivalent of "officer". Doble and vire both mean "turn". Mire ("look") is used to get someone's attention. Queda is often used instead of está for where a place is.
