The sources have few sightseeing phrases, so this file is short; place descriptions are summarised in our own words. Pronunciations follow Spain (z, ce, ci = "th").

## Asking for recommendations

- ¿Dónde hay muchos sitios para visitar? [DOHN-deh AY MOO-chohs SEE-tyohs PAH-rah bee-see-TAR] = Where are there a lot of sights to see?
- ¿Dónde hay muchos restaurantes? [DOHN-deh AY MOO-chohs rehs-tow-RAHN-tehs] = Where are there a lot of restaurants?
- ¿Dónde hay muchos bares? [DOHN-deh AY MOO-chohs BAH-rehs] = Where are there a lot of bars?
- ¿Dónde está el centro de información? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl THEHN-troh deh een-for-mah-THYOHN] = Where is the information centre?
- ¿Puede enseñarme en el mapa? [PWEH-deh ehn-seh-NYAR-meh ehn ehl MAH-pah] = Can you show me on the map?
- ¿Cómo puedo llegar al centro? [KOH-moh PWEH-doh yeh-GAR ahl THEHN-troh] = How do I get to the centre?
- ¿Hay alguna especialidad de la zona? [AY ahl-GOO-nah ehs-peh-thyah-lee-DAHD deh lah THOH-nah] = Is there a local specialty?

## Planning a trip

- Tengo ganas de ir a Segovia. [TEHN-goh GAH-nahs deh eer ah seh-GOH-byah] = I feel like going to Segovia.
- ¿Qué haces este fin de semana? [keh AH-thehs EHS-teh feen deh seh-MAH-nah] = What are you doing this weekend? (informal)
- ¿Estás libre este fin de semana? [ehs-TAHS LEE-breh EHS-teh feen deh seh-MAH-nah] = Are you free this weekend? (informal)
- Quiero reservar un pasaje de ida y vuelta a ... [KYEH-roh rreh-sehr-BAR oon pah-SAH-heh deh EE-dah ee BWEHL-tah ah] = I want to book a return ticket to ... (Peace Corps, Uruguay. For trains and buses in Spain the ticket is un billete.)
- (agent) ¿A qué hora le gustaría viajar? [ah keh OH-rah leh goos-tah-REE-ah byah-HAR] = What time would you like to travel?
- la agencia de viajes = the travel agency
- Mañana por la mañana. [mah-NYAH-nah pohr lah mah-NYAH-nah] = Tomorrow morning.

## Opening and closing

- ¿Cuándo cierran? [KWAHN-doh THYEH-rrahn] = When do you close? / When is closing time?
- ABIERTO [ah-BYEHR-toh] = open
- CERRADO [theh-RRAH-doh] = closed
- ENTRADA [ehn-TRAH-dah] = entrance
- SALIDA [sah-LEE-dah] = exit
- PROHIBIDO [proh-ee-BEE-doh] = forbidden
- NO FUMAR [noh foo-MAR] = no smoking
- SERVICIOS / ASEOS = toilets
  On the 14 national holidays shops, offices and banks close, but bars and restaurants stay open (FSI Cultural Notes). Many shops close for a few hours at lunchtime. The sources give no current museum opening hours: check each museum before you go.

## Saying what you think

- ¿Te gusta Toledo? [teh GOOS-tah toh-LEH-doh] = Do you like Toledo? (informal)
- Me encanta. [meh ehn-KAHN-tah] = I love it.
- Nos encanta, especialmente Madrid. [nohs ehn-KAHN-tah, ehs-peh-thyahl-MEHN-teh mah-DREETH] = We love it, especially Madrid.
- Sí, nos gusta mucho. [SEE, nohs GOOS-tah MOO-choh] = Yes, we like it a lot.
- Esto es precioso. [EHS-toh ehs preh-THYOH-soh] = This is beautiful.
- Muy bonito. [MOO-ee boh-NEE-toh] = Very pretty.

## Famous places in Madrid and nearby

- el Museo del Prado = one of the finest art museums in the world, strong in Spanish painters such as Velázquez and Goya
- el Reina Sofía = Madrid's modern art museum; el Thyssen-Bornemisza = a large private collection of classical art. With the Prado they form the museum district along the Paseo del Prado.
- la Puerta del Sol = the busy central square, the heart of Madrid
- la Plaza Mayor = the best known square in Madrid, with arcades, shops and cafés
- el Palacio Real = the Royal Palace, one of the biggest in Europe, used for state ceremonies
- el Parque del Buen Retiro = Madrid's main park
- El Escorial = the royal monastery northwest of Madrid, burial place of Spanish kings
- Segovia = famous for its Roman aqueduct, built without mortar
- Ávila = medieval town walls with 88 towers
- Toledo = old walled city full of art and architecture, with a Gothic cathedral and the Alcázar

## Famous places in the rest of Spain

- Barcelona: the Gothic Quarter, Gaudí's Church of the Sacred Family (begun in 1882), the Ramblas (tree-lined avenues from the harbour to the centre), the Picasso Museum
- Granada: la Alhambra (the Moorish palace) and the Sacromonte hill, known for flamenco
- Sevilla: the cathedral and la Giralda tower, side by side
- Córdoba: la Mezquita (the former great mosque, now a cathedral) and the old Jewish Quarter
- Burgos: the Gothic cathedral where El Cid is buried
- Valencia: home of paella and of the Fallas festival in March
- The Balearic Islands (Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza, Formentera) and the Costa del Sol: beaches and sunshine
- Santiago de Compostela: pilgrims travel to the tomb of St James (Saint James' Day, 25 July)

## Festivals

- la fiesta = a holiday or festival (most towns celebrate their patron saint's day)
Holy Week (Easter) brings processions, especially in Seville, Valladolid and Burgos.
- las Fallas = Valencia's festival around 19 March, when giant figures are burned
On 6 January (Epiphany) the Three Kings bring children's presents.
- el Rastro = Madrid's Sunday-morning flea market (stalls close around 2 pm)

## Sightseeing words (Libro Libre)

Source: Erin Huebener, "Libro Libre: Beginning Spanish" (2018), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (this section is shared under the same licence), "Las vacaciones" (p. 311). English glosses are ours.

- la guía turística = guidebook;  el plano = (city) map;  el folleto = brochure
- la catedral = cathedral;  el centro histórico = old town;  el monumento = monument
- comprar recuerdos = to buy souvenirs;  ir de compras = to go shopping
- el mar = sea;  la playa = beach;  el campo = countryside;  acampar = to camp
- ir de vacaciones = to go on holiday;  estar de vacaciones = to be on holiday
- el piso / la planta = floor (of a building);  el ascensor = lift;  las escaleras = stairs
