# Spanish for getting around: bus, metro, taxi and car

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Spain" (1984), Module II Units 1-4 (tickets, petrol station, car repair, "¡Taxi, taxi!") and the "Cultural Notes" (transportation), https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-headstart-for-spain-course/. CC BY-SA 4.0. ## Ways to travel
Country: ES (Spain)

- el autobús [ow-toh-BOOS] = the bus (ómnibus in Uruguay, guagua in the Caribbean)
- el metro [MEH-troh] = the metro / underground
- el tren [trehn] = the train
- el taxi [TAHK-see] = the taxi
- el coche [KOH-cheh] = the car (Spain. Carro in Latin America; in Spain a carro is a cart. Auto is understood everywhere.)
- el avión [ah-BYOHN] = the plane; el barco [BAR-koh] = the ship / boat; el tranvía [trahn-BEE-ah] = the tram
- la bicicleta / la bici [bee-thee-KLEH-tah / BEE-thee] = the bicycle; la moto [MOH-toh] = the motorbike
  In Spain "to take" a bus or train is coger (coger el autobús). In Latin America use tomar, because coger is vulgar there.

## Bus and metro
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¿A qué hora sale el autobús para Toledo? [ah keh OH-rah SAH-leh ehl ow-toh-BOOS PAH-rah toh-LEH-doh] = What time does the bus to Toledo leave?
- ¿Cuándo llega el autobús? [KWAHN-doh YEH-gah ehl ow-toh-BOOS] = When does the bus arrive?
- ¿Puede decirme a qué hora llega el autobús? [PWEH-deh deh-THEER-meh ah keh OH-rah YEH-gah ehl ow-toh-BOOS] = Can you tell me what time the bus arrives?
- ¿Adónde va este autobús? [ah-DOHN-deh bah EHS-teh ow-toh-BOOS] = Where does this bus go?
- ¿Se para este autobús en ...? [seh PAH-rah EHS-teh ow-toh-BOOS ehn] = Does this bus stop in ...?
- ¿Hay una estación de autobuses cerca de aquí? [AY OO-nah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh ow-toh-BOO-sehs THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Is there a bus station near here?
- (local) Pasan cada 20 minutos. [PAH-sahn KAH-dah BAYN-teh mee-NOO-tohs] = They come every 20 minutes.
- la parada = the stop; la estación de autobuses = the bus station
  Long-distance buses in Spain are cheaper than trains and reach places trains don't. Cities usually have one bus station used by all companies.

## Tickets
Country: ES (Spain)

- Un billete de ida para Toledo. [oon bee-YEH-teh deh EE-dah PAH-rah toh-LEH-doh] = A one-way ticket to Toledo.
- Un billete de ida y vuelta para Zaragoza. [oon bee-YEH-teh deh EE-dah ee BWEHL-tah PAH-rah thah-rah-GOH-thah] = A return ticket to Zaragoza.
- ¿Cuánto cuesta un billete a Sevilla? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah oon bee-YEH-teh ah seh-BEE-yah] = How much is a ticket to Seville?
- ¿Cuánto cuesta? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah] = How much is it?
- el billete = ticket (Spain); el pasaje = ticket (South America, also for plane and boat); el boleto = ticket (Mexico, Central America, Uruguay)
- de ida = one-way; de ida y vuelta = return / round trip
  More on train tickets: see trains.md.

## Taxi
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¡Taxi! [TAHK-see] = Taxi!
- Está libre, ¿no? [ehs-TAH LEE-breh, noh] = You're free, aren't you?
- (driver) Sí, señor. [SEE, seh-NYOR] = Yes, sir.
- Al aeropuerto, por favor. [ahl ah-eh-roh-PWEHR-toh, pohr fah-BOHR] = To the airport, please.
- Al Hotel Plaza, por favor. [ahl oh-TEHL PLAH-thah, pohr fah-BOHR] = To the Hotel Plaza, please.
- A la estación de Chamartín, por favor. [ah lah ehs-tah-THYOHN deh chah-mar-TEEN, pohr fah-BOHR] = To Chamartín station, please.
- Lléveme a ..., por favor. [YEH-beh-meh ah ..., pohr fah-BOHR] = Take me to ..., please.
- ¿Cuánto cuesta ir a ...? [KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah eer ah] = How much does it cost to go to ...?
## Taxi: luggage, speed and paying
Country: ES (Spain)

- (driver) ¿Tiene equipaje? [TYEH-neh eh-kee-PAH-heh] = Do you have any luggage?
- Sólo una maleta. [SOH-loh OO-nah mah-LEH-tah] = Only one suitcase.
- No, no tengo equipaje. [noh, noh TEHN-goh eh-kee-PAH-heh] = No, I don't have any luggage.
- ¿Puede ir un poco más rápido? Tengo prisa. [PWEH-deh eer oon POH-koh mahs RRAH-pee-doh. TEHN-goh PREE-sah] = Can you go a little faster? I'm in a hurry.
- ¿Puede ir más despacio? [PWEH-deh eer mahs dehs-PAH-thyoh] = Can you go slower?
- Pare aquí, por favor. [PAH-reh ah-KEE, pohr fah-BOHR] = Stop here, please.
- Pare en la próxima esquina, por favor. [PAH-reh ehn lah PROHK-see-mah ehs-KEE-nah, pohr fah-BOHR] = Stop at the next corner, please.
- Déjeme ahí, por favor. [DEH-heh-meh ah-EE, pohr fah-BOHR] = Leave me there, please.
- ¿Cuánto le debo? [KWAHN-toh leh DEH-boh] = How much do I owe you?
- Tome. Quédese con el cambio. [TOH-meh. KEH-deh-seh kohn ehl KAHM-byoh] = Here you are. Keep the change.
  A free taxi shows a LIBRE sign behind the windscreen, and at night a green light on top. In the city taxis use the meter; for trips outside the city agree the fare first. Luggage may cost extra.

## At the airport
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¿Dónde está el control de pasaportes? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH ehl kohn-TROHL deh pah-sah-POR-tehs] = Where is passport control?
- (officer) ¿Tiene algo para declarar? [TYEH-neh AHL-goh PAH-rah deh-klah-RAR] = Do you have anything to declare?
- No tengo nada para declarar. [noh TEHN-goh NAH-dah PAH-rah deh-klah-RAR] = I have nothing to declare.
- ¿Dónde están las llegadas? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAHN lahs yeh-GAH-dahs] = Where are the arrivals?
- ¿Dónde está la puerta ...? [DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah PWEHR-tah] = Where is gate ...?
- ¿A qué hora sale el próximo avión para Valencia? [ah keh OH-rah SAH-leh ehl PROHK-see-moh ah-BYOHN PAH-rah bah-LEHN-thyah] = What time does the next plane to Valencia leave?

## Hiring a car and driving
Country: ES (Spain)

- Quiero alquilar un coche. [KYEH-roh ahl-kee-LAR oon KOH-cheh] = I want to rent a car.
- ¿Cuánto es diario? [KWAHN-toh ehs DYAH-ryoh] = How much is it per day?
- ¿Eso incluye seguro? [EH-soh een-KLOO-yeh seh-GOO-roh] = Does that include insurance?
- ¿Esta carretera llega a ...? [EHS-tah kah-rreh-TEH-rah YEH-gah ah] = Does this road lead to ...?
- ¿Cuánto tiempo puedo estacionarme aquí? [KWAHN-toh TYEHM-poh PWEH-doh ehs-tah-thyoh-NAR-meh ah-KEE] = How long can I park here? (Spanish signs say aparcar / aparcamiento; estacionar is the Latin American word.)
- ¿Hay un aparcamiento cerca de aquí? [AY oon ah-par-kah-MYEHN-toh THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Is there a car park near here?
- STOP = stop (on Spanish signs; ALTO or PARE in Latin America); CEDA EL PASO = give way; NO APARCAR = no parking; SENTIDO ÚNICO = one way; PELIGRO = danger; DESVÍO = diversion

## Petrol station and breakdowns

- Póngame veinte litros, por favor. [POHN-gah-meh BAYN-teh LEE-trohs, pohr fah-BOHR] = Put in twenty litres, please.

- ¿Qué le debo? [keh leh DEH-boh] = What do I owe you?
## Car breakdowns and repairs
Country: ES (Spain)

- ¿Hay un taller mecánico cerca de aquí? [AY oon tah-YEHR meh-KAH-nee-koh THEHR-kah deh ah-KEE] = Is there a car repair shop near here?
- ¿Me podría arreglar el coche? [meh poh-DREE-ah ah-rreh-GLAR ehl KOH-cheh] = Could you fix my car?
- (mechanic) ¿Qué es lo que tiene? [keh ehs loh keh TYEH-neh] = What's wrong with it?
- A veces no arranca. [ah BEH-thehs noh ah-RRAHN-kah] = Sometimes it doesn't start.
- Se calienta mucho y se para. [seh kah-LYEHN-tah MOO-choh ee seh PAH-rah] = It overheats a lot and stalls.
- Necesito un mecánico. [neh-theh-SEE-toh oon meh-KAH-nee-koh] = I need a mechanic.
- (mechanic) Hoy no tengo tiempo. Tráigalo mañana. [OY noh TEHN-goh TYEHM-poh. TRAY-gah-loh mah-NYAH-nah] = I don't have time today. Bring it tomorrow.
- ¿Puedo traerlo mañana por la mañana? [PWEH-doh trah-EHR-loh mah-NYAH-nah pohr lah mah-NYAH-nah] = Can I bring it tomorrow morning?
- la gasolinera = petrol station; la gasolina = petrol; el gasóleo / el diésel = diesel; el aceite = oil; los frenos = brakes; la batería = battery; el motor = engine

## Taking a taxi and agreeing the fare (Latin America)
Country: 419 (Unspecified Latin America)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Latin America Headstart Course", Module II Unit 4 "Taking a Taxi" (pp. 106-108); set in Panama City, https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-latin-america. US Government work (DLI); no license statement in the PDF. English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- ¿Está libre? [ehs-TAH LEE-breh] = Are you free? (to a taxi driver)
- ¿Por cuánto me lleva al Hotel Soloy? [pohr KWAHN-toh meh YEH-bah ahl oh-TEHL soh-LOY] = How much to take me to the Soloy Hotel? (Use with any place: ¿Por cuánto me lleva a...?)
- ¿Tanto? [TAHN-toh] = That much?
- (driver) Ese hotel queda muy lejos de aquí. [EH-seh oh-TEHL KEH-dah mooy LEH-hohs deh ah-KEE] = That hotel is very far from here.
- ¿No me lleva por tres dólares? [noh meh YEH-bah pohr trehs DOH-lah-rehs] = Won't you take me for three dollars?
- (driver) Bueno, está bien. ¿Tiene equipaje? [BWEH-noh, ehs-TAH byehn. TYEH-neh eh-kee-PAH-heh] = Well, OK. Do you have any luggage?
- Solamente tengo dos maletas pequeñas. [soh-lah-MEHN-teh TEHN-goh dohs mah-LEH-tahs peh-KEH-nyahs] = I only have two small suitcases.
  In the course's notes (1980s), Panama City had two kinds of taxi: one with fixed prices that waits at hotel and public-building taxi stands, and one that only drives inside the city and will bargain over the fare. Some drivers charged extra for suitcases. Check current practice before you travel.

## What the taxi driver says (Mexico)
Country: MX (Mexico)

Source: drafted for this app and checked by the project's Spanish reviewer (2026-11-24). Pronunciation guides are not given for these lines yet.

- (driver) Buenas tardes. ¿A dónde te llevo? = Good afternoon. Where can I take you? (informal tú; the same for anyone)
- (driver) ¿A dónde lo llevo? = Where can I take you? (formal usted, to a man; to a woman: ¿A dónde la llevo?)
- (driver) Súbase, por favor. = Get in, please. (formal usted)
- (driver) Súbete, por favor. = Get in, please. (informal tú)
- (driver) Son ... pesos. = It's ... pesos.
- (driver) Llegamos en ... minutos. = We'll be there in ... minutes.
- (driver) ¿Le ayudo con las maletas? = Can I help you with your bags? (formal usted)
- (driver) ¿Te ayudo con las maletas? = Can I help you with your bags? (informal tú)
- (driver) Ya llegamos. = We're here.
  Note: tú or usted depends on how formal the situation is. A driver may use usted with an older passenger or someone they do not know, and tú with a young or familiar one, or when the passenger asks to be called tú. Whichever form is chosen, it stays the same for the whole conversation.

## Car, gas station and repairs (Latin America)
Country: 419 (Unspecified Latin America)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Latin America Headstart Course", Module II Unit 2 "Is There a Gas Station Near Here?" and Unit 3 "Is There an Auto Shop Near Here?" (pp. 74-76, 94-96) and Module II Glossary (pp. 116-124); set in Panama, https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-latin-america. - el carro [ehl KAH-rroh] = the car
- el bus [ehl boos] = the bus
- la lancha [lah LAHN-chah] = the ferry (boat)
- ¡Me llena el tanque, por favor! [meh YEH-nah ehl TAHN-keh, pohr fah-BOHR] = Fill the tank, please!
- Me chequea la batería. [meh cheh-KEH-ah lah bah-teh-REE-ah] = Will you check the battery for me?

  Gas station is la gasolinera; you will also hear estación de gas and estación de gasolina.

## Públicos, guaguas and ferries (Puerto Rico)
Country: PR (Puerto Rico)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Puerto Rico", Module II Unit 1 "Taking a Public Car" (conversation and notes pp. 59-60, exercises pp. 61-64, key pp. 211-213) and Module II Glossary (p. 111), https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-puerto-rico. US Government work (DLI); no license statement in the PDF. English glosses and pronunciations are ours.

- el carro público / el público [ehl KAH-rroh POO-blee-koh / ehl POO-blee-koh] = the public car (shared car or van between towns)
- la guagua [lah GWAH-gwah] = the bus
- la parada de guaguas [lah pah-RAH-dah deh GWAH-gwahs] = the bus stop
- el terminal de guaguas [ehl tehr-mee-NAHL deh GWAH-gwahs] = the bus terminal
- la lancha [lah LAHN-chah] = the ferry
- Perdone, señora. ¿Dónde están los carros públicos? [pehr-DOH-neh, seh-NYOH-rah. DOHN-deh ehs-TAHN lohs KAH-rrohs POO-blee-kohs] = Excuse me, ma'am. Where are the public cars?
- En la plaza, señor. [ehn lah PLAH-sah, seh-NYOR] = In the plaza, sir.
- ¿A qué hora sale la guagua para Bayamón? [ah KEH OH-rah SAH-leh lah GWAH-gwah PAH-rah bah-yah-MOHN] = At what time does the bus leave for Bayamón?
- ¿Cuándo llega la guagua a Carolina? [KWAHN-doh YEH-gah lah GWAH-gwah ah kah-roh-LEE-nah] = When does the bus arrive in Carolina?
- ¿A qué hora llega la lancha a Cataño? [ah KEH OH-rah YEH-gah lah LAHN-chah ah kah-TAH-nyoh] = At what time does the ferry arrive in Cataño?
- ¿Va a salir el público pronto? [BAH ah sah-LEER ehl POO-blee-koh PROHN-toh] = Is the público going to leave soon?
- Chofer: Salgo pronto. Como en diez minutos. [SAHL-goh PROHN-toh. KOH-moh ehn DYEHS mee-NOO-tohs] = I'm leaving soon. In about ten minutes.
- Viajero: ¿Cuándo sale usted para Fajardo? [KWAHN-doh SAH-leh oos-TEHD PAH-rah fah-HAR-doh] = When do you leave for Fajardo?
- Viajero: ¿Y cuánto cuesta? [ee KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah] = And how much is it?
  The carro público (often just el público) is privately owned and licensed as public transport between towns. It usually leaves from the main plaza, destinations and fares are fixed, and it often waits until it is full before leaving. (Course note; the course is undated, so check current details.)
  Bus stops are marked by yellow posts or signs that say Parada or Parada de Guaguas.

## Driving: the gas station and the garage (Puerto Rico)
Country: PR (Puerto Rico)

Source: Defense Language Institute, "Spanish Headstart for Puerto Rico", Module II Unit 2 "The Gas Station" (conversation and notes pp. 75-76) and Unit 3 "At the Auto Shop" (conversation and notes pp. 93-94, key p. 219), https://www.livelingua.com/course/fsi/spanish-headstart-for-puerto-rico. - ¡Llene el tanque, por favor! [YEH-neh ehl TAHN-keh, pohr fah-BOHR] = Fill the tank, please!
- Chequée también el agua del radiador y el aceite, por favor. [cheh-KEH-eh tahm-BYEHN ehl AH-gwah dehl rah-dyah-DOR ee ehl ah-SAY-teh, pohr fah-BOHR] = Please also check the radiator water and the oil.

- (staff) ¿Qué tiene? [KEH TYEH-neh] = What's wrong with it?
- Se ahoga, y a veces no prende. [seh ah-OH-gah, ee ah BEH-sehs noh PREHN-deh] = It stalls, and sometimes it doesn't start.
- (staff) Déjeme chequear el motor. [DEH-heh-meh cheh-keh-AR ehl moh-TOR] = Let me check the engine.
- (staff) Tráigalo mañana. [TRAY-gah-loh mah-NYAH-nah] = Bring it tomorrow.
- ¿Puede arreglar los frenos? [PWEH-deh ah-rreh-GLAR lohs FREH-nohs] = Can you fix the brakes?
- ¿Puede chequear las luces? [PWEH-deh cheh-keh-AR lahs LOO-sehs] = Can you check the headlights?
- las gomas [lahs GOH-mahs] = the tires
  Puerto Rico uses United States currency. Prices are often said without dólares or centavos, and chavos is also used for cents.

## Taking the micro (bus) (Paraguay)
Country: PY (Paraguay)

Source: Peace Corps, "Spanish I and Work Book (For Peace Corps Volunteers)", Unidad Seis, "En la parada" and vocabulary (pp. 64-66), https://www.livelingua.com/course/peace-corps/spanish-language-lessons---paraguay. Peace Corps (US Government) training material; the ERIC copy states it is federally funded or otherwise in the public domain.
