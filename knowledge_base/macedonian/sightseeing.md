## Planning a trip or tour
- Како можам да патувам до ...? [Kako mozham da patuvam do ...?] [KA-ko MO-zham da PA-too-vam do ...?] = How can I travel to ...?
- Со авион? Со воз? Со автобус? Со кола? Со брод? [So avion? So voz? So avtobus? So kola? So brod?] [so A-vee-on? so VOZ? so AV-to-boos? so KO-la? so BROD?] = By plane? By train? By bus? By car? By boat?
- За колку дена е патувањето? [Za kolku dena e patuvanjeto?] [za KOL-koo DE-na e pa-too-VA-nye-to?] = How many days is the trip?
- Колку чини патувањето? [Kolku chini patuvanjeto?] [KOL-koo CHEE-nee pa-too-VA-nye-to?] = How much does the trip cost?
- Кое е најевтино / најскапо? [Koe e najevtino / najskapo?] [KO-e e na-YEV-tee-no / NAY-ska-po?] = Which is the cheapest / most expensive? (the book spells it најефтино)
- Кое патување најмногу ви се допаѓа? Зошто? [Koe patuvanje najmnogu vi se dopagja? Zoshto?] [KO-e pa-TOO-va-nye NAY-mno-goo vee se DO-pa-gya? ZOSH-to?] = Which trip do you like most? Why?
- Каде има посета и на други места? [Kade ima poseta i na drugi mesta?] [KA-de EE-ma PO-se-ta ee na DROO-gee MES-ta?] = Where are there visits to other places too?

## Tour and travel words
- патување [patuvanje] [pa-TOO-va-nye] trip, патува [patuva] [PA-too-va] to travel, излет [izlet] [EEZ-let] excursion / day trip, посета [poseta] [PO-se-ta] visit
- разгледување на градот [razgleduvanje na gradot] [raz-gle-DOO-va-nye na GRA-dot] city sightseeing tour, пловење со брод [plovenje so brod] [PLO-ve-nye so BROD] boat trip
- ноќевање [nokjevanje] [no-KYE-va-nye] overnight stay (четири дена [chetiri dena] [CHE-tee-ree DE-na] / две ноќевања [dve nokjevanja] [DVE no-KYE-va-nya]: 4 days / 2 nights)
- вечера во традиционален ресторан [vechera vo tradicionalen restoran] [VE-che-ra vo tra-dee-tsee-O-na-len RES-to-ran] dinner in a traditional restaurant
- воз [voz] [VOZ] train, авион [avion] [A-vee-on] plane, брод [brod] [BROD] boat / ship, чамец [chamec] [CHA-mets] / кајче [kajche] [KAY-che] small boat

## Famous places in North Macedonia (from Kramer, Lesson 16)
- Охрид (Ohrid): a lakeside town; a popular excursion goes from Ohrid to Свети Наум (Sveti Naum), a monastery on the lake.
- Скопје (Skopje): the capital. The old bazaar, чаршија, is the old part of town for strolling. The art gallery is in Даут-пашиниот амам, a former Turkish bath, with works by Macedonian painters such as Коџоман, Личеноски and Мартиноски.
- Водно (Vodno): the mountain above Skopje, going up to Пантелејмон (St Panteleimon monastery). From above you can see the city stretched along the river.
- Езерото Матка (Lake Matka): near Skopje. You can take a small boat to the cave Врело, one of the deepest underwater caves in the world. Beautiful nature.
- Курбиново (Kurbinovo): its church of Св. Ѓорѓија (St George) has 12th-century frescoes.
- Брајчино (Brajchino): a village that has become a centre of eco-tourism, with tourists from all over the world.
- Битола (Bitola): a major city in the south-west.

## Skopje: Sights in the city center

- Плоштад Македонија [Ploshtad Makedonija] [PLOSH-tad ma-ke-DO-nee-ya] = Macedonia Square, the main square, with a large statue and fountain in the middle
- Камен мост [Kamen most] [KA-men MOST] = Stone Bridge, an old bridge over the river Vardar that links Macedonia Square with the Old Bazaar
- Стара скопска чаршија [Stara skopska charshija] [STA-ra SKOP-ska CHAR-shee-ya] = Old Bazaar, one of the largest old bazaars in the Balkans, with mosques, an old hammam, small shops, cafés and kebapchinja grills
- Скопско Кале [Skopsko Kale] [SKOP-sko KA-le] = Skopje Fortress, on a hill above the Old Bazaar, with a view over the whole city
- Меморијален дом на Мајка Тереза [Memorijalen dom na Majka Tereza] [me-mo-REE-ya-len DOM na MAY-ka TE-re-za] = Memorial House of Mother Teresa, who was born in Skopje in 1910
- Стара железничка станица [Stara zheleznichka stanica] [STA-ra zhe-LEZ-neech-ka STA-nee-tsa] = Old Railway Station, now the City Museum; its clock stopped at 5:17, the time of the 1963 earthquake
- Градски парк [Gradski park] [GRAD-skee PARK] = City Park, a large green park along the Vardar, good for walking and running

## Skopje: Near Skopje
- Водно [Vodno] [VOD-no] = Vodno, the mountain above the city; a cable car (жичница) goes up to the Millennium Cross (Милениумски крст)
- Кањон Матка [Kanjon Matka] [KA-nyon MAT-ka] = Matka Canyon, about 17 km from the center, with a lake, kayaks, boat trips to a cave, old monasteries and hiking paths

## Skopje: Culture notes
The center is easy to walk: you can cross the Stone Bridge from Macedonia Square and be in the Old Bazaar in five minutes. The Old Bazaar is the best place for traditional food, coffee and souvenirs. Matka is a popular half-day trip; go early on summer weekends because it gets busy.

## Ohrid and Struga: Ohrid old town
- Охридско Езеро [Ohridsko Ezero] [O-hreed-sko E-ze-ro] = Lake Ohrid, one of the oldest and deepest lakes in Europe, on the UNESCO World Heritage list
- Стариот град [Stariot grad] [STA-ree-ot GRAD] = the Old Town of Ohrid, with narrow stone streets, old houses and churches
- Св. Јован Канео [Sv. Jovan Kaneo] [SV. YO-van KA-ne-o] = Church of St. John at Kaneo, a small church on a cliff above the lake, the most famous view of Ohrid
- Самуилова тврдина [Samuilova tvrdina] [sa-moo-EE-lo-va TVR-dee-na] = Samuel's Fortress, on the top of the old town, with a view over the lake
- Антички театар [Antichki teatar] [AN-teech-kee TE-a-tar] = Ancient Theatre, used for concerts and plays during the Ohrid Summer Festival
- Плаошник [Plaoshnik] [PLA-osh-neek] = Plaoshnik, where St. Clement's church and his old school stand
- Св. Софија [Sv. Sofija] [SV. SO-fee-ya] = Church of St. Sophia, a medieval church with old frescoes

## Ohrid and Struga: Around the lake
- Св. Наум [Sv. Naum] [SV. NA-oom] = St. Naum Monastery, about 30 km south of Ohrid, near the springs of the river Crn Drim; you can take a small rowing boat on the springs
- Залив на коските [Zaliv na koskite] [ZA-leev na KOS-kee-te] = Bay of Bones, a museum on the water with rebuilt prehistoric houses on wooden posts
- Струга [Struga] [STROO-ga] = Struga, a town where the river Crn Drim leaves the lake, known for the Struga Poetry Evenings
- охридски бисер [ohridski biser] [O-hreed-skee BEE-ser] = Ohrid pearl, a traditional handmade souvenir

## Ohrid and Struga: Culture notes
Ohrid is the most popular summer destination in the country, so book accommodation early for July and August. The walk along the lake from the old town to Kaneo is short and beautiful, especially at sunset. Boats to St. Naum leave from the Ohrid harbor in summer; outside summer it's easier to go by bus or taxi.

## Around North Macedonia: Towns

## Around North Macedonia: Culture notes
The country is small: most places are two to three hours by car or bus from Skopje, so day trips are easy. Many national parks and mountain villages are best visited from late spring to early autumn; in winter Mavrovo is a ski area.

## Bitola and Pelister: Sights in Bitola
- Битола [Bitola] [BEE-to-la] = Bitola, a large town in the south-west near the Greek border, called "the city of consuls" (градот на конзулите) because many countries had consulates here in the 19th century
- Широк сокак [Shirok sokak] [SHEE-rok SO-kak] = Shirok Sokak, the long pedestrian street full of cafés, old houses and people walking in the evening
- Саат-кула [Saat-kula] [SA-at-KOO-la] = the Clock Tower, a symbol of the town, near the center
- Битолска чаршија [Bitolska charshija] [BEE-tol-ska CHAR-shee-ya] = Bitola's Old Bazaar, with small shops and the covered market (Безистен)
- Хераклеја Линкестис [Herakleja Linkestis] [he-RA-kle-ya LEEN-kes-tees] = Heraclea Lynkestis, an ancient city just outside Bitola, founded by Philip II of Macedon, famous for its floor mosaics and theatre

## Bitola and Pelister: Culture notes
People in Bitola are proud of their town's history and its "korzo" culture: in the evening everyone walks up and down Shirok Sokak and sits in the cafés. Bitola is about two and a half to three hours from Skopje by bus. Pelister is only about 15 km away, so you can stay in a village guesthouse and hike the next morning.

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: Culture notes
Wine making in Tikvesh is a family tradition, and many families still make homemade wine and rakija. The Tikvesh winery in Kavadarci is one of the oldest and largest in the region. In September the whole region smells of grapes during the harvest, and the Kavadarci festival is the best time to visit. Remember that the driver should not drink: the legal limit for drivers is very low.

## Things to do: Things to do
- пливање во Охридското Езеро [plivanje vo Ohridskoto Ezero] [PLEE-va-nye vo o-HREED-sko-to E-ze-ro] = swimming in Lake Ohrid (summer)
- возење со брод [vozenje so brod] [VO-ze-nye so BROD] = a boat ride, for example from Ohrid to St. Naum
- кајак во Матка [kajak vo Matka] [KA-yak vo MAT-ka] = kayaking in Matka Canyon
- планинарење [planinarenje] [pla-nee-NA-re-nye] = hiking, for example on Vodno, Pelister or in Mavrovo
- дегустација на вино [degustacija na vino] [de-goos-TA-tsee-ya na VEE-no] = wine tasting in the Tikvesh region
- параглајдинг во Крушево [paraglajding vo Krushevo] [pa-RA-glay-deeng vo KROO-she-vo] = paragliding in Krushevo
- скијање [skijanje] [SKEE-ya-nye] = skiing in Mavrovo or Popova Shapka (winter)
- шетање низ Старата чаршија [shetanje niz Starata charshija] [SHE-ta-nye neez STA-ra-ta CHAR-shee-ya] = walking through the Old Bazaar in Skopje
- шетање во стариот град на Охрид [shetanje vo stariot grad na Ohrid] [SHE-ta-nye vo STA-ree-ot GRAD na O-hreed] = walking through Ohrid's old town

## Things to do: Festivals and holidays
- Охридско лето [Ohridsko leto] [O-hreed-sko LE-to] = Ohrid Summer Festival, concerts and theatre in July and August
- Струшки вечери на поезијата [Strushki vecheri na poezijata] [STROOSH-kee VE-che-ree na po-e-ZEE-ya-ta] = Struga Poetry Evenings, an international poetry festival in late August
- Скопски џез фестивал [Skopski dzhez festival] [SKOP-skee JEZ FES-tee-val] = Skopje Jazz Festival, in October
- Вевчански карневал [Vevchanski karneval] [VEV-chan-skee KAR-ne-val] = Vevchani Carnival, in mid-January, with masks and costumes
- Илинден [Ilinden] [EE-leen-den] = Ilinden, a national holiday on 2 August
- Ден на независноста [Den na nezavisnosta] [DEN na ne-za-VEES-nos-ta] = Independence Day, 8 September

## Things to do: Useful phrases
- Што може да се прави овде? [Shto mozhe da se pravi ovde?] [SHTO MO-zhe da SE pra-vee OV-de?] = What can you do here?
- Каде може да купам билети? [Kade mozhe da kupam bileti?] [KA-de MO-zhe da KOO-pam BEE-le-tee?] = Where can I buy tickets?
- Колку чини влезницата? [Kolku chini vleznicata?] [KOL-koo CHEE-nee vlez-NEE-tsa-ta?] = How much is the entrance ticket?
- До колку часот е отворено? [Do kolku chasot e otvoreno?] [do KOL-koo CHA-sot e ot-VO-re-no?] = Until what time is it open?
- Дали има водич на англиски? [Dali ima vodich na angliski?] [DA-lee EE-ma VO-deech na AN-glees-kee?] = Is there a guide in English?
- Колку трае турата? [Kolku trae turata?] [KOL-koo TRA-e TOO-ra-ta?] = How long does the tour take?
- Дали може да се фотографира? [Dali mozhe da se fotografira?] [DA-lee MO-zhe da se fo-to-GRA-fee-ra?] = Can you take photos?
- Сакам да резервирам за двајца. [Sakam da rezerviram za dvajca.] [SA-kam da re-ZER-vee-ram za DVAY-tsa.] = I want to book for two people.

## Things to do: Sample dialogue
- Добар ден. Дали има брод до Св. Наум денес? [Dobar den. Dali ima brod do Sv. Naum denes?] [DO-bar DEN. DA-lee EE-ma BROD do SV. NA-oom DE-nes?] = Hello. Is there a boat to St. Naum today?
- Продавачот: Да, бродот тргнува во десет часот. [Da, brodot trgnuva vo deset chasot.] [DA, BRO-dot TRG-noo-va vo DE-set CHA-sot.] = Seller: Yes, the boat leaves at ten o'clock.
- Колку чини билетот? [Kolku chini biletot?] [KOL-koo CHEE-nee BEE-le-tot?] = How much is the ticket?
- Продавачот: Петстотини денари, во двата правци. [Petstotini denari, vo dvata pravci.] [pet-STO-tee-nee DE-na-ree, vo DVA-ta PRAV-tsee.] = Seller: Five hundred denars, return.
- Колку трае патувањето? [Kolku trae patuvanjeto?] [KOL-koo TRA-e pa-too-VA-nye-to?] = How long is the trip?
- Продавачот: Околу еден час во еден правец. [Okolu eden chas vo eden pravec.] [O-ko-loo E-den CHAS vo E-den PRA-vets.] = Seller: About one hour one way.
- Добро, два билета, ве молам. [Dobro, dva bileta, ve molam.] [DO-bro, DVA BEE-le-ta, VE mo-lam.] = OK, two tickets, please.
- Продавачот: Повелете. Убаво поминување! [Povelete. Ubavo pominuvanje!] [po-VE-le-te. OO-ba-vo po-mee-NOO-va-nye!] = Seller: Here you go. Have a nice time!

## Things to do: Culture notes
Summer (June to September) is the season for the lakes and festivals; many boat trips run only in summer. Museums and churches often charge a small entrance fee in denars, and some ask you to cover your shoulders inside churches. Prices in the sample dialogue are examples only.
