# Macedonian for sightseeing, tours and famous places

Source: Christina E. Kramer & Liljana Mitkovska, "Macedonian: A Course for Beginning and Intermediate Students", 3rd ed., University of Wisconsin Press, 2011, Lesson 9 (tour adverts and questions, pp. 195-196) and Lesson 16 (Cultural Sites, letter "Спомени", pp. 381-382). Place descriptions summarised in our own words; English glosses ours.

## Planning a trip or tour
- Како можам да патувам до ...? = How can I travel to ...?
- Со авион? Со воз? Со автобус? Со кола? Со брод? = By plane? By train? By bus? By car? By boat?
- За колку дена е патувањето? = How many days is the trip?
- Колку чини патувањето? = How much does the trip cost?
- Кое е најевтино / најскапо? = Which is the cheapest / most expensive? (the book spells it најефтино)
- Кое патување најмногу ви се допаѓа? Зошто? = Which trip do you like most? Why?
- Каде има посета и на други места? = Where are there visits to other places too?

## Tour and travel words
- патување trip, патува to travel, излет excursion / day trip, посета visit
- разгледување на градот city sightseeing tour, пловење со брод boat trip
- ноќевање overnight stay (4 дена / 2 ноќевања = 4 days / 2 nights)
- вечера во национален ресторан dinner in a traditional restaurant
- воз train, авион plane, брод boat / ship, чамец small boat

## Famous places in North Macedonia (from Kramer, Lesson 16)
- Охрид (Ohrid): a lakeside town; a popular excursion goes from Ohrid to Свети Наум (Sveti Naum), a monastery on the lake.
- Скопје (Skopje): the capital. The old bazaar, чаршија, is the old part of town for strolling. The art gallery is in Даут-пашиниот амам, a former Turkish bath, with works by Macedonian painters such as Коџоман, Личеноски and Мартиноски.
- Водно (Vodno): the mountain above Skopje, going up to Пантелејмон (St Panteleimon monastery). From above you can see the city stretched along the river.
- Езерото Матка (Lake Matka): near Skopje. You can take a small boat to the cave Врело, one of the deepest underwater caves in the world. Beautiful nature.
- Курбиново (Kurbinovo): its church of Св. Ѓорѓија (St George) has 12th-century frescoes.
- Брајчино (Brajčino): a village that has become a centre of eco-tourism, with tourists from all over the world.
- Битола (Bitola): a major city in the south-west.

## Useful sightseeing sentences
- Шетавме по чаршијата. = We strolled through the old bazaar.
- Утре одиме на излет до Свети Наум. = Tomorrow we're going on an excursion to Sveti Naum.
- Со чамец отидовме до пештерата. = We went to the cave by boat.
- Ги разгледавме фреските. = We looked at the frescoes.
- Таму има многу убава природа. = There's very beautiful nature there.
- црква church, манастир monastery, пештера cave, езеро lake, река river, планина mountain, фрески frescoes, галерија gallery, туристи tourists

## Skopje: Sights in the city center
Source for this and the following sections: team-authored notes, written and reviewed by a native Macedonian speaker (Marija), November 2026.
- Плоштад Македонија [Ploshtad Makedonija] = Macedonia Square, the main square, with a large statue and fountain in the middle
- Камен мост [Kamen most] = Stone Bridge, an old bridge over the river Vardar that links Macedonia Square with the Old Bazaar
- Стара скопска чаршија [Stara skopska charshija] = Old Bazaar, one of the largest old bazaars in the Balkans, with mosques, an old hammam, small shops, cafés and kebapchinja grills
- Скопско Кале [Skopsko Kale] = Skopje Fortress, on a hill above the Old Bazaar, with a view over the whole city
- Меморијален дом на Мајка Тереза [Memorijalen dom na Majka Tereza] = Memorial House of Mother Teresa, who was born in Skopje in 1910
- Стара железничка станица [Stara zheleznichka stanica] = Old Railway Station, now the City Museum; its clock stopped at 5:17, the time of the 1963 earthquake
- Градски парк [Gradski park] = City Park, a large green park along the Vardar, good for walking and running

## Skopje: Near Skopje
- Водно [Vodno] = Vodno, the mountain above the city; a cable car (жичница) goes up to the Millennium Cross (Милениумски крст)
- Кањон Матка [Kanjon Matka] = Matka Canyon, about 17 km from the center, with a lake, kayaks, boat trips to a cave, old monasteries and hiking paths

## Skopje: Useful phrases
- Каде е Камениот мост? [Kade e Kameniot most?] = Where is the Stone Bridge?
- Како да стигнам до Старата чаршија? [Kako da stignam do Starata charshija?] = How do I get to the Old Bazaar?
- Може ли пешки до таму? [Mozhe li peshki do tamu?] = Can I walk there?
- Што да видам во Скопје? [Shto da vidam vo Skopje?] = What should I see in Skopje?
- Колку чини жичницата за Водно? [Kolku chini zhichnicata za Vodno?] = How much is the cable car to Vodno?
- Како да стигнам до Матка? [Kako da stignam do Matka?] = How do I get to Matka?

## Skopje: Culture notes
The center is easy to walk: you can cross the Stone Bridge from Macedonia Square and be in the Old Bazaar in five minutes. The Old Bazaar is the best place for traditional food, coffee and souvenirs. Matka is a popular half-day trip; go early on summer weekends because it gets busy.

## Ohrid and Struga: Ohrid old town
- Охридско Езеро [Ohridsko Ezero] = Lake Ohrid, one of the oldest and deepest lakes in Europe, on the UNESCO World Heritage list
- Стариот град [Stariot grad] = the Old Town of Ohrid, with narrow stone streets, old houses and churches
- Св. Јован Канео [Sv. Jovan Kaneo] = Church of St. John at Kaneo, a small church on a cliff above the lake, the most famous view of Ohrid
- Самуилова тврдина [Samuilova tvrdina] = Samuel's Fortress, on the top of the old town, with a view over the lake
- Антички театар [Antichki teatar] = Ancient Theatre, used for concerts and plays during the Ohrid Summer Festival
- Плаошник [Plaoshnik] = Plaoshnik, where St. Clement's church and his old school stand
- Св. Софија [Sv. Sofija] = Church of St. Sophia, a medieval church with old frescoes

## Ohrid and Struga: Around the lake
- Св. Наум [Sv. Naum] = St. Naum Monastery, about 30 km south of Ohrid, near the springs of the river Crn Drim; you can take a small rowing boat on the springs
- Залив на коските [Zaliv na koskite] = Bay of Bones, a museum on the water with rebuilt prehistoric houses on wooden posts
- Струга [Struga] = Struga, a town where the river Crn Drim leaves the lake, known for the Struga Poetry Evenings
- охридски бисер [ohridski biser] = Ohrid pearl, a traditional handmade souvenir

## Ohrid and Struga: Useful phrases
- Каде е црквата Св. Јован Канео? [Kade e crkvata Sv. Jovan Kaneo?] = Where is the Church of St. John at Kaneo?
- Како да стигнам до Св. Наум? [Kako da stignam do Sv. Naum?] = How do I get to St. Naum?
- Дали има брод до Св. Наум? [Dali ima brod do Sv. Naum?] = Is there a boat to St. Naum?
- Може ли да се плива овде? [Mozhe li da se pliva ovde?] = Can you swim here?
- Каде може да купам охридски бисер? [Kade mozhe da kupam ohridski biser?] = Where can I buy an Ohrid pearl?

## Ohrid and Struga: Culture notes
Ohrid is the most popular summer destination in the country, so book accommodation early for July and August. The walk along the lake from the old town to Kaneo is short and beautiful, especially at sunset. Boats to St. Naum leave from the Ohrid harbor in summer; outside summer it's easier to go by bus or taxi.

## Around North Macedonia: Towns
- Прилеп [Prilep] = Prilep, a town known for tobacco and for Marko's Towers (Маркови кули), rocks and fortress ruins above the town
- Крушево [Krushevo] = Krushevo, one of the highest towns in the Balkans, known for paragliding and for the Ilinden Uprising of 1903
- Кратово [Kratovo] = Kratovo, a small town inside an old volcano crater, with medieval towers and stone bridges
- Струмица [Strumica] = Strumica, a town in the south-east, known for its carnival and fresh vegetables

## Around North Macedonia: Nature
- Маврово [Mavrovo] = Mavrovo National Park, with a lake, a half-sunken church, and skiing in winter
- Преспанско Езеро [Prespansko Ezero] = Lake Prespa, a quiet lake known for apples and the island Golem Grad
- Дојранско Езеро [Dojransko Ezero] = Lake Dojran, a small warm lake in the south-east

## Around North Macedonia: History
- Стоби [Stobi] = Stobi, ruins of an ancient city next to the highway between Skopje and Gevgelija
- Кокино [Kokino] = Kokino, a very old megalithic observatory on a hill in the north-east

## Around North Macedonia: Useful phrases
- Колку е далеку Битола од Скопје? [Kolku e daleku Bitola od Skopje?] = How far is Bitola from Skopje?
- Што препорачувате да видам? [Shto preporachuvate da vidam?] = What do you recommend I see?
- Дали вреди да се оди таму? [Dali vredi da se odi tamu?] = Is it worth going there?
- Дали има автобус до Маврово? [Dali ima avtobus do Mavrovo?] = Is there a bus to Mavrovo?

## Around North Macedonia: Culture notes
The country is small: most places are two to three hours by car or bus from Skopje, so day trips are easy. Many national parks and mountain villages are best visited from late spring to early autumn; in winter Mavrovo is a ski area.

## Bitola and Pelister: Sights in Bitola
- Битола [Bitola] = Bitola, a large town in the south-west near the Greek border, called "the city of consuls" (градот на конзулите) because many countries had consulates here in the 19th century
- Широк сокак [Shirok sokak] = Shirok Sokak, the long pedestrian street full of cafés, old houses and people walking in the evening
- Саат-кула [Saat-kula] = the Clock Tower, a symbol of the town, near the center
- Битолска чаршија [Bitolska charshija] = Bitola's Old Bazaar, with small shops and the covered market (Безистен)
- Хераклеја Линкестис [Herakleja Linkestis] = Heraclea Lynkestis, an ancient city just outside Bitola, founded by Philip II of Macedon, famous for its floor mosaics and theatre

## Bitola and Pelister: Nature near Bitola
- Пелистер [Pelister] = Pelister National Park, the mountain above Bitola, with the molika pine (молика), a rare tree that grows here
- Пелистерски очи [Pelisterski ochi] = "Pelister's eyes", two mountain lakes near the top of Pelister, a full-day hike
- Дихово [Dihovo] = Dihovo, a village at the foot of Pelister with guesthouses and home-cooked food

## Bitola and Pelister: Festivals
- Браќа Манаки [Brakja Manaki] = Manaki Brothers International Film Festival, a festival of cinematography held in Bitola every autumn

## Bitola and Pelister: Useful phrases
- Каде е Широк сокак? [Kade e Shirok sokak?] = Where is Shirok Sokak?
- Колку е далеку Хераклеја од центарот? [Kolku e daleku Herakleja od centarot?] = How far is Heraclea from the center?
- Може ли пешки до Хераклеја? [Mozhe li peshki do Herakleja?] = Can I walk to Heraclea?
- Сакам да одам на Пелистер. [Sakam da odam na Pelister.] = I want to go to Pelister.
- Колку е долга патеката до езерата? [Kolku e dolga patekata do ezerata?] = How long is the trail to the lakes?

## Bitola and Pelister: Sample dialogue
- Извинете, што да видам во Битола за еден ден? [Izvinete, shto da vidam vo Bitola za eden den?] = Excuse me, what should I see in Bitola in one day?
- Битолчанката: Прошетајте по Широк сокак и испијте кафе. Потоа одете во Хераклеја, близу е. [Proshetajte po Shirok sokak i ispijte kafe. Potoa odete vo Herakleja, blizu e.] = Local woman: Walk along Shirok Sokak and have a coffee. Then go to Heraclea, it's close.
- Може ли пешки до таму? [Mozhe li peshki do tamu?] = Can I walk there?
- Битолчанката: Да, околу дваесет минути пешки. [Da, okolu dvaeset minuti peshki.] = Local woman: Yes, about twenty minutes on foot.
- А Пелистер? [A Pelister?] = And Pelister?
- Битолчанката: За Пелистер ви треба цел ден. Одете рано наутро. [Za Pelister vi treba cel den. Odete rano nautro.] = Local woman: For Pelister you need a whole day. Go early in the morning.
- Благодарам многу! [Blagodaram mnogu!] = Thank you very much!

## Bitola and Pelister: Culture notes
People in Bitola are proud of their town's history and its "korzo" culture: in the evening everyone walks up and down Shirok Sokak and sits in the cafés. Bitola is about two and a half to three hours from Skopje by bus. Pelister is only about 15 km away, so you can stay in a village guesthouse and hike the next morning.

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: The region
- Тиквеш [Tikvesh] = Tikvesh, the biggest and best-known wine region in North Macedonia, in the center-south of the country along the river Vardar
- Кавадарци [Kavadarci] = Kavadarci, the main town of the Tikvesh region and the center of wine making, about 1.5 hours from Skopje
- Неготино [Negotino] = Negotino, a nearby wine town
- Демир Капија [Demir Kapija] = Demir Kapija, a gorge on the Vardar ("iron gate"), with wineries and rocks for climbing
- Тиквешко Езеро [Tikveshko Ezero] = Tikvesh Lake, a large lake near Kavadarci, surrounded by hills
- Полошки манастир [Poloshki manastir] = Polog Monastery (St. George), an old monastery on the shore of Tikvesh Lake, reached by boat

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: Wine
- винарија [vinarija] = winery
- винова лоза [vinova loza] = grapevine
- гроздобер [grozdober] = grape harvest, in September
- Вранец [Vranec] = Vranec, the most famous Macedonian red grape and wine, dark and strong
- Темјаника [Temjanika] = Temjanika, an aromatic white wine
- Смедеревка [Smederevka] = Smederevka, a common white grape
- црвено / бело / розе вино [crveno / belo / roze vino] = red / white / rosé wine
- суво / слатко вино [suvo / slatko vino] = dry / sweet wine
- ракија [rakija] = rakija

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: Festivals
- Тиквешки гроздобер [Tikveshki grozdober] = Tikvesh Grape Harvest Festival in Kavadarci, in early September, with a parade, music and free wine tasting

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: Sample dialogue
- Водичот: Добредојдовте во винаријата! Ова е нашиот Вранец. [Dobredojdovte vo vinarijata! Ova e nashiot Vranec.] = Guide: Welcome to the winery! This is our Vranec.
- Од која година е ова вино? [Od koja godina e ova vino?] = What year is this wine from?
- Водичот: Од 2020 година. Пробајте. [Od 2020 godina. Probajte.] = Guide: From 2020. Try it.
- Многу е добро! Дали имате и бело вино? [Mnogu e dobro! Dali imate i belo vino?] = It's very good! Do you also have white wine?
- Водичот: Да, Темјаника. Таа е лесна и мирисна. [Da, Temjanika. Taa e lesna i mirisna.] = Guide: Yes, Temjanika. It's light and aromatic.
- Би сакал да купам две шишиња Вранец. [Bi sakal da kupam dve shishinja Vranec.] = I would like to buy two bottles of Vranec.
- Водичот: Повелете. На здравје! [Povelete. Na zdravje!] = Guide: Here you go. Cheers!

## Tikvesh and Kavadarci: Culture notes
Wine making in Tikvesh is a family tradition, and many families still make homemade wine and rakija. The Tikvesh winery in Kavadarci is one of the oldest and largest in the region. In September the whole region smells of grapes during the harvest, and the Kavadarci festival is the best time to visit. Remember that the driver should not drink: the legal limit for drivers is very low.

## Things to do: Festivals and holidays
- Охридско лето [Ohridsko leto] = Ohrid Summer Festival, concerts and theatre in July and August
- Струшки вечери на поезијата [Strushki vecheri na poezijata] = Struga Poetry Evenings, an international poetry festival in late August
- Скопски џез фестивал [Skopski dzhez festival] = Skopje Jazz Festival, in October
- Вевчански карнeвал [Vevchanski karneval] = Vevchani Carnival, in mid-January, with masks and costumes
- Илинден [Ilinden] = Ilinden, a national holiday on 2 August
- Ден на независноста [Den na nezavisnosta] = Independence Day, 8 September

## Things to do: Sample dialogue
- Добар ден. Дали има брод до Св. Наум денес? [Dobar den. Dali ima brod do Sv. Naum denes?] = Hello. Is there a boat to St. Naum today?
- Продавачот: Да, бродот тргнува во десет часот. [Da, brodot trgnuva vo deset chasot.] = Seller: Yes, the boat leaves at ten o'clock.
- Колку чини билетот? [Kolku chini biletot?] = How much is the ticket?
- Продавачот: Петстотини денари, во двата правци. [Petstotini denari, vo dvata pravci.] = Seller: Five hundred denars, return.
- Колку трае патувањето? [Kolku trae patuvanjeto?] = How long is the trip?
- Продавачот: Околу еден час во еден правец. [Okolu eden chas vo eden pravec.] = Seller: About one hour one way.
- Добро, два билета, ве молам. [Dobro, dva bileta, ve molam.] = OK, two tickets, please.
- Продавачот: Повелете. Убаво поминување! [Povelete. Ubavo pominuvanje!] = Seller: Here you go. Have a nice time!

## Things to do: Culture notes
Summer (June to September) is the season for the lakes and festivals; many boat trips run only in summer. Museums and churches often charge a small entrance fee in denars, and some ask you to cover your shoulders inside churches. Prices in the sample dialogue are examples only.
