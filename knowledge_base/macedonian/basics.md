# Macedonian basics: alphabet, greetings, introductions

Source: Peace Corps Macedonia, "Macedonian Language / Makedonski Jazik" (For Invitees), Lessons 1-4, 7, 9.
https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/macedonian/courses/peace-corps-macedonian-language-lessons/

## Alphabet and stress
Macedonian uses a form of the Cyrillic alphabet with 31 letters; each letter is one sound. There are 5 vowels: а, е, и, о, у.
Stress: in words of one, two or three syllables, stress falls on the first syllable. In longer words it falls on the third syllable from the end.
Letters English speakers often miss: ѓ (gy, as in "argue"), ќ (ky, as in "cute"), ѕ (dz, as in "beds"), љ (ly), њ (ny, as in "canyon"), џ (j, as in "jungle"), ј (y, as in "yard"), ж (zh, as in "pleasure").

## Yes, no, and understanding each other
- Да. [Da.] [DA.] = Yes.  Не. [Ne.] [NE.] = No.
- Дали зборувате англиски? [Dali zboruvate angliski?] [DA-lee zbo-ROO-va-te AN-glees-kee?] = Do you speak English?
- Малку. [Malku.] [MAL-koo.] = A little.
- Дали разбирате? [Dali razbirate?] [DA-lee raz-BEE-ra-te?] = Do you understand?
- Не разбирам. [Ne razbiram.] [ne RAZ-bee-ram.] = I don't understand.
- Не знам. [Ne znam.] [NE znam.] = I don't know.
- Ве молам, зборувајте побавно. [Ve molam, zboruvajte pobavno.] [VE mo-lam, zbo-ROO-vay-te PO-bav-no.] = Please, speak slowly.
- Како се вика ... на македонски? [Kako se vika ... na makedonski?] [KA-ko SE vee-ka ... na ma-KE-don-skee?] = How do you say ... in Macedonian?
- Што е ова? [Shto e ova?] [SHTO e O-va?] = What is this?  Ова е... [Ova e...] [O-va e...] = This is...

## Polite words
- Благодарам. [Blagodaram.] [bla-GO-da-ram.] = Thank you.
- Извинете. [Izvinete.] [eez-VEE-ne-te.] = Excuse me. / Sorry.
- Ве молам. [Ve molam.] [VE mo-lam.] = Please.
- Повелете. [Povelete.] [po-VE-le-te.] = Here you are. / May I help you?

## Days of the week
Понеделник [Ponedelnik] [po-NE-del-neek] Monday, Вторник [Vtornik] [VTOR-neek] Tuesday, Среда [Sreda] [SRE-da] Wednesday, Четврток [Chetvrtok] [CHE-tvr-tok] Thursday, Петок [Petok] [PE-tok] Friday, Сабота [Sabota] [SA-bo-ta] Saturday, Недела [Nedela] [NE-de-la] Sunday.

## Greetings and introductions: Sample dialogue
- Добар ден! [Dobar den!] [DO-bar DEN!] = Good afternoon!
- Домаќинот: Добар ден! Повелете. [Dobar den! Povelete.] [DO-bar DEN! po-VE-le-te.] = Host: Good afternoon! Come in / How can I help?
- Јас се викам Ана. Зборувате ли англиски? [Jas se vikam Ana. Zboruvate li angliski?] [YAS SE vee-kam A-na. zbo-roo-VA-te lee AN-glees-kee?] = My name is Ana. Do you speak English?
- Домаќинот: Малку. Мило ми е, Ана. Од каде сте? [Malku. Milo mi e, Ana. Od kade ste?] [MAL-koo. MEE-lo mee e, A-na. od KA-de ste?] = Host: A little. Nice to meet you, Ana. Where are you from?
- Од Америка. Зборувам малку македонски. [Od Amerika. Zboruvam malku makedonski.] [od a-ME-ree-ka. ZBO-roo-vam MAL-koo ma-KE-don-skee.] = I am from America. I speak a little Macedonian.
- Домаќинот: Браво! Добредојдовте во Македонија! [Bravo! Dobredojdovte vo Makedonija!] [BRA-vo! do-bre-DOY-dov-te vo ma-ke-DO-nee-ya!] = Host: Well done! Welcome to Macedonia!

## Greetings and introductions: Culture notes
Use "Вие" (formal you) and "Добар ден" with strangers, older people and staff; "Здраво" and "ти" are for friends and young people. "Повелете" is a very common, polite word meaning roughly "here you go", "come in" or "how can I help you". People appreciate it when visitors try even a few words of Macedonian.
